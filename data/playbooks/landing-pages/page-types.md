# Page type recipes

Section order for each page type. Recipes are starting points: drop a section the client can't support (no
consented B&As, no membership plan) rather than fill it with placeholders, and say so in NOTES.md. Recipes as
built for Siha (Oct 2026) are in `outputs/creative/siha-lp/pages/*.json`.

## New patient (assessment / check-up offer)
header · hero_form (priced assessment) · awards · usp_text · story_cards · pricing (panel) · membership (if the
practice has plans) · google_reviews · form_band · pain_points ("it's been a while") · explainer (what the
assessment covers) · steps · services (photo cards) · why_us · meet_dentist · team · practice_tour · faq · final_cta
Thank-you: confirm + online booking.

## Emergency dentist
header · hero_form (fixed fee, same-day wording the client confirms) · awards · usp_text · story_cards (emergency
reviews first) · pricing (panel: fee, what's included, out-of-hours fee) · google_reviews · form_band ("don't wait in
pain") · pain_points (symptoms) · explainer (what counts as an emergency) · steps (what happens when you get in
touch) · services (what we treat, icons) · why_us (opening hours as a reason) · meet_dentist · team ·
practice_tour · faq · final_cta
Thank-you: call back promise; call + WhatsApp buttons only if the client asks (tracked); online booking.

## Smile makeover
header · hero_form (free consultation, "from £x a tooth") · awards · usp_text · story_cards · pricing (cards:
bonding, aligners, veneers + finance strip) · spotlight (the clinic's real differentiator, if any) · results (B&A
carousel) · google_reviews · form_band (with deposit note if the client takes one) · pain_points · explainer (what a
smile makeover is) · steps · services (building blocks, icons) · why_us · meet_dentist · team · practice_tour · faq ·
final_cta
Thank-you: confirm consultation + online booking.

## Copy sources, in order
1. Toby's draft on the LP theme (the hero price stands)
2. Facts the client confirmed (calls in Fathom, emails)
3. The client's website fees/treatment pages (with the date checked)
4. Client Bio sheet (offers, hooks, opening hours)
Never: other clinics' copy, stock claims, or figures nobody can source.
