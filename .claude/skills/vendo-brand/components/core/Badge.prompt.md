Small status pill — communicates state with colour, never emoji.

```jsx
<Badge tone="positive" dot>Active</Badge>
<Badge tone="negative">Overdue</Badge>
<Badge tone="info">Draft ready</Badge>
```

- `tone`: `positive` · `warning` · `negative` · `info` · `accent` · `neutral`.
- `dot` adds a leading status dot. Keep labels one or two words.
