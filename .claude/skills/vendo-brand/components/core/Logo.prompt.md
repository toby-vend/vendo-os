The Vendo wordmark rendered live in Manrope — handy for UI chrome. On `white` tone the trailing dot is mint (the signature).

```jsx
<Logo tone="white" size={26} />
<Logo variant="icon" tone="green" size={32} />
```

- `variant`: `wordmark` ("Vendo.") · `icon` ("V.").
- `tone`: `white` (dark bg) · `green` · `black` (light bg).
- For print, favicons, or pixel-exact use, reference the official SVGs in `assets/logo/` instead.
