The creative is the targeting.

How paid social works in 2026 — and the system we run.

The shift

Media buying is no longer the edge

  • Targeting is automated — broad is the default
  • Meta's Andromeda update decides distribution by reading the creative itself
  • The old hacks (duplicating ad sets, bid tricks) are dead

The edge moved to creative — and most teams are still bad at it. That gap is ours to take.

The shift

Run a portfolio, never heroes

Every ad fatigues. Every ad has a spend ceiling. So spread the load:

Brands built on two or three hero creatives arrive 40% down year-on-year when they all fatigue together. The fix happens six months before the problem shows.

The algorithm

The creative you load in IS the targeting

Before

You picked the audience, then added creative.

Now

Meta reads the ad — transcript, copy, visuals — and finds the people for it.

A vague ad gives Meta nothing to target with. An ad clearly for one person tells Meta exactly who to find.

Meta auto-transcribes every ad — the opening line mechanically steers who sees it.

The algorithm

The last-click trap

Meta sequences ads across a journey — then puts all the credit on the last click.

AD 1 · WARMS
shows 1.4x
→
AD 2 · EDUCATES
shows 2x
→
AD 3 · CLOSES
shows 6x — takes all the credit

Never switch off an ad inside an ad set that's hitting KPI. Killing ads 1 and 2 destroys the sequence that made ad 3 work.

Trust numbers at campaign level (7-day click), roughly at ad-set level, never at ad level. For dental, GHL is the source of truth — leads, booked consults, treatment value.

Concepts

Concept = persona × angle × offer

Persona

Who we're talking to — and who's on screen

Angle

The argument: which pain, what proof, what belief we challenge

Offer

Product + value framing: consult, bundle, finance

Format is a weak fourth lever — it comes last, never first. Diversity means changing at least one of the three.

Structural rule: one concept per ad set. Mixed concepts confuse targeting and make results unreadable.

Concepts

Personas are problems, not demographics

"Women 25–45" is not a persona.

"The professional who hides their smile on video calls and has been thinking about Invisalign for two years" — that's a persona. You can already see the script.

IMAGE 2
Two stills from real client ads showing clearly different personas on screen (e.g. young professional vs 60+ patient)

2–4 core personas per client, built manually from real pain points — then AI expands the list, never the other way round.

Hooks

The hook is most of the ad

80%
of viewers never watch past the first 3 seconds
15 + 2
15 hooks on 2 bodies beats 2 hooks on 5 bodies — the performance delta lives in the hook
  • Grade hooks on: clarity · relevance · novelty · specificity · credibility
  • Agitate the problem — persona call-outs ("if you're a mum of two…") are overrated
  • Two criteria done well = a good hook

Lakewood · Implant Ad 1 — one ad body, four hook variants shot in one session (three shown, frames from the opening seconds via Frame.io)

Hooks

Introduce the offer as late as possible

A great hook then an instant sales pitch ruins the asset. Later product introduction = more qualified viewers = more total conversions, not fewer.

Hooks

Hook swaps are nearly free spend

  • A new hook = a new creative ID to Meta — fresh audience, not bundled with the original
  • Fatigued winners + new hooks have delivered another $100K of spend at the same efficiency for an hour of shooting

Shoot rule

Every shoot captures multiple hook takes per concept. Standard line on every shot list from now on.

Formats

Three format opinions the data backs

  • Statics are underrated — cheap, fast, and "statics only work bottom-of-funnel" is a skill issue. Long-copy statics crush with older Facebook audiences — exactly the dental patient base
  • Partnership ads — content through the clinician's or creator's own profile borrows their audience and doesn't feel like an ad
  • DPA warning (ecomm) — catalogue ROAS flatters heavily; check incremental attribution before shifting budget into it

Production mix does the funnel maths now: ~70–90% of what we make is top-of-funnel.

The rules

The rules card

Always

  • One concept per ad set
  • Judge at campaign level, 7-day click
  • Cross-check against GHL
  • New ads for a winner → new ad set
  • Hook-swap fatigued winners
  • Multiple hook takes at every shoot

Never

  • Kill an ad in an ad set hitting KPI
  • Trust ad-level ROAS or CPL
  • Inject ads into a winning ad set
  • Duplicate ad sets to "scale"
  • Test format before angle
  • Let a client ride one or two hero ads
