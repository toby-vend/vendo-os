For scale: the entire moulding head-term set the site is chasing is 157,400 searches a month. LVT and laminate alone are 159,000.

For
  • The drop-ship model removes stock risk and picking labour entirely
  • The supplier is across the road — fulfilment friction is close to nil
  • Genuine adjacency: the same renovation buyer, at the same moment in the job
  • Difficulty is low on several of the head terms
Against
  • A different competitive set, with national retailers already in it
  • Paid CPCs run £1.00–£2.00, well above current moulding costs
  • No brand equity or authority in flooring to build on
  • Margin is the supplier's to set, not yours

Recommendation

Worth a contained trial, but judged on its own funnel and its own P&L — not folded into the moulding forecast, where it would flatter the numbers without proving anything. Decide after wave one of the hardwood launch has landed, so it competes for attention against a lever we already know works rather than against a blank quarter. That sequencing is why it carries a £0 floor in the gap model.
