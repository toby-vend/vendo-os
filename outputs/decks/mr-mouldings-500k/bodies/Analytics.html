Direct sessions went from roughly 19,000 a month to 466,756 in thirty days — a 24-fold rise — while search, social and orders all stayed on trend. Traffic with no referrer, at that volume, with no corresponding revenue, is automated. The September mobile QA independently logged 429 rate-limit responses from a Microsoft Ads agent endpoint on ordinary page loads.

Why it matters beyond the reporting

  • Every conversion rate in Shopify Analytics is wrong by a factor of ten, so any decision taken on that number — ad budgets, channel comparisons, judging whether a change worked — is taken on corrupted data
  • It contaminates the signal fed back to Google and Meta for optimisation. This needs stopping before any new paid budget is set
  • It is real server load. The performance work attributed its missed page-speed gates partly to server response time, and half a million extra monthly sessions is a plausible contributor
