The 301% case includes the step-change off a small base and is almost certainly not sustainable. The 62% figure is the honest forward number — and it lands almost exactly on the timeline in your brief.

On the last six months' rate the business reaches £500,000 a month in about 14 months without doing anything new. So every initiative in this deck should be judged on whether it beats a 62% baseline — not on whether it is exciting.
