For comparison, "torus skirting" alone takes 5,200 searches a month. The specification audience is not absent — it simply does not arrive through search.

Do not build SEO for this audience. The £8,900 tulipwood orders in the order book are the proof of concept: this channel already converts when it finds you. It needs to be sold to, not ranked for.

What the specification channel actually needs

  • An NBS Source listing. The platform architects and specifiers use to write products into build documents. This is the single highest-leverage item on the list
  • Sample boxes. A physical, branded set that sits on a studio shelf. Specifiers choose from what is in front of them
  • CAD and detail drawings. DWG profiles and NBS-ready specification clauses, downloadable per profile. Without these the product cannot be drawn into a job
  • Named outbound. Architects and interior designers working on period and high-end residential, approached directly — not through a form
  • A trade contact route. Somebody who answers a specification question in an hour, not a general enquiry inbox

The economics

One specified job at the current tulipwood average of £8,900 is worth twenty ordinary orders. Three of them in sixty days arrived with no marketing at all. This is a low-volume, high-value channel where the cost of acquisition is a sample box and a conversation, and it simply needs an owner.
