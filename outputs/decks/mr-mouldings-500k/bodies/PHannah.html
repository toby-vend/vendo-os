In one line

Lives in a new build that feels bland and wants to give it character. Buying a look rather than fixing a problem.

What they buy

  • Taller skirting to replace the builder-grade 95mm, plus panelling and architrave to match.
  • Whole-room transformations rather than single fixes.

What stops them

  • Does not realise the skirting is the thing making the room feel cheap.
  • Assumes changing it is a builder job.

Hook angles

  • The reason your new build feels like a rental.
  • Swap this one thing and the whole room grows up.
  • Developer skirting versus what it should have been.
