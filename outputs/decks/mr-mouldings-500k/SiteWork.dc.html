One release, not nine projects

The live theme has not changed since 14 July. The development branch is 168 changes ahead of it. Every item marked merged goes live in a single approved release; wave 5 follows once it has been reviewed on its preview.

Why wave 5 matters

It targets the moment of decision on a phone: trust visible without scrolling, checkout always in reach, and price per metre (median £15.79) so lengths compare like for like.

Still to build

Cookie banner and Klaviyo targeting, Trustpilot placement, and eight smaller items awaiting a design decision. The configurator's admin tool and publishing engine follow the Phase 1 sign-off.
