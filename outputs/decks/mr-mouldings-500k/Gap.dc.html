How to read these numbers honestly

These levers are not additive on top of the 62% trend — much of that growth arrives through them. Stacking them to a headline total would overstate the case. The honest claim is narrower and more useful: this plan protects the 62% baseline and gives a credible route to twelve months or better, with the first three items costing almost nothing because the work is either already built or simply absent.

The order matters more than the list

Stop the bots, then release the built work, then fix the paid structure — in that sequence. Each one makes the measurement of the next trustworthy. Adding budget or products before them means spending into a funnel that mis-reports its own performance, which is how a 62% trend quietly becomes a 30% one.
