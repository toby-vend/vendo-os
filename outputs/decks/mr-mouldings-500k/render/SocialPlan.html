08 · Paid social · The plan

One campaign, a creative engine, and new customers first

Four moves, sequenced behind the bot fix and the site release so the account learns from clean signal on a site that converts.

01 · Q4 · Weeks 1–4

Clean the signal

  • Stop the bot traffic before any budget moves
  • Rebuild retargeting pools from add-to-cart and purchase events only; page-view audiences are polluted
  • Audit Conversions API deduplication
  • Optimise to the new-versus-returning purchase split now set up in the account

02 · Q4

Consolidate

  • Four prospecting campaigns become one Advantage+ sales campaign, value-optimised, with an existing-customer budget cap
  • One catalogue retargeting campaign
  • Launches ring-fenced in their own campaigns so they cannot starve the core
  • Returning buyers handed to email

03 · From 30 Sep · Monthly

A creative engine

  • 8–12 new ads a month. One new ad has spent anything since January
  • Iterate the proven winners first: lookbook statics and the Amelia UGC
  • Then installed-in-situ builder content, Adam's renovation before and after, founder-led profile explainers
  • The 30 September content day produces the first batch

04 · Q1 – Q2

New lines, own campaigns

  • Solid hardwood launch, Georgian wave first, on its own budget
  • Trade account acquisition aimed at builders and designers, once the trade structure is live
  • Pinterest trial in Q2, judged on its own funnel and kept out of the forecast

Why the site release matters more to Meta than to any other channel

Meta sends mobile shoppers straight to product and collection pages. On the live site, size options take 45 seconds to become usable on a phone and Add to cart does nothing before options are chosen. Every pound of social spend lands on the pages the built work fixes, so no scaling happens before the release.

What we are not doing

No new audiences stacked on the old structure, no budget into ads older than 90 days without a refreshed variant, and no paid follower growth. Reach comes from content that sells.
