Paid creative strategy

Audience division: trade and homeowner

Paid speaks to two different buyers. Trade buys on spec and consistency, homeowners on how the finished room will look, so each gets its own creative.

B2B

Trade

What they are buying

Consistent machining order after order, the right grade for each job (primed, moisture resistant, fire rated), a made to match service, and a supplier who knows the product.

What paid has to prove

That the spec stays the same from one order to the next, and that the team can answer the technical questions properly.

D2C

Homeowner

What they are buying

A room that looks more expensive than it cost, with a profile that suits the house.

What paid has to prove

That it is achievable by a normal person in a weekend, and that the finish looks right.
