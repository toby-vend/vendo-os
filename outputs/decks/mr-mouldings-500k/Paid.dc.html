Same products, same feed, same season. The difference is data density: split eight ways, no single campaign accumulates enough conversions for smart bidding to optimise.

Brand is flattering the blend

Brand search returns 30.87× on £1,183 — demand you already own. Strip it out and working media returns 5.20×, not the headline 6.40×.

The marginal pound is buying less

Spend rose 44% from June to August. Revenue rose 14%. The incremental £7,704 returned 2.65× — against a 6.40× blended average.

The plan, before any new budget

  • Consolidate all Shopping into a single campaign; fold the six sub-£500 campaigns into their parents
  • Restructure Search into fewer campaigns with more ad groups, so budgets and data pool
  • Strip generic keyword insertion from the RSAs; pin real differentiators — made to measure, 3–4 day lead time — and mine customer reviews for copy
  • Scale Microsoft Ads from £3.2k to its £5.4k ceiling at 7–8× ROAS, weighted to panel moulds and cornices
