## 4. UGC — TOF framing (per agreed strategy: UGC introduces, other ads close)

The agreed role for UGC is top-of-funnel: it earns attention and brings people in; the purchase then closes on a later ad (DPA/retargeting) which takes the attribution. Judged on that basis, per-ad ROAS is the wrong yardstick — and the funnel data supports the mechanism:

- **Click→add-to-cart is identical to the rest of the account (9%).** UGC visitors shop like everyone else once they land. The drop is ATC→purchase (15% vs 21% on other sales ads) — UGC-opened carts close later, and Meta credits the closing ad, not the introduction. That is the attribution transfer working exactly as designed: the DPA/catalogue layer that closes ran at 372% on 50K DKK Jul–Aug.
- **Attention is where UGC clearly wins:** thumbstop 17% vs 5.3% account average, CTR ~1.9–3% vs 1.7%. This is the "people need to see it" job.
- **Honest nuance (internal):** as a pure traffic buy it is not cheap — cost per landed visitor 3.71 DKK vs 2.86 for other sales ads; cost per ATC 32.7 vs 27.3. Its edge is attention quality, not click economics. And at current delivery it cannot do its TOF job at all: 959 DKK (~£105) in all of August, ~2% of spend, because Advantage+ throttles it on last-click signals — the structural bias against TOF creative inside a sales campaign.
- **Attributed floor anyway:** lifetime 4,255 DKK spend, 236% ROAS, 20 purchases — even on last-click it more than pays for itself; the TOF contribution is on top of that floor.
- **Recommendation:** measure UGC on TOF KPIs (cost per landed visitor, cost per ATC, thumbstop/hook rate, new reach) plus a halo check (blended prospecting+retargeting ROAS holding or improving as UGC spend scales) — and report those in the weekly so Stuart sees it in the numbers. To fund the TOF job properly, give it a dedicated ad set/budget (~150–200 DKK/day) rather than letting Advantage+ starve it. Note the planned UGC shoot is still blocked by the Trade Holster 32" waist stockout (30 Jul call).

## 6. Tactics to drag ROAS to 5x

The pruning maths alone gets most of the way there:

- August blended: 368%.
- Remove the two weak creative-test prospecting sets (8,743 DKK at ~90% blended): remaining account ran at **425%**.
- Remove awareness (3,873 DKK, no direct revenue): **469%** on the sales layer alone.

So the plan:
1. **Prune:** cut or rebuild "Prospecting | Creatives" and "Prospecting | Video Creatives" — 8.7K DKK (~£960) at sub-130% in August is the single biggest ROAS lever.
2. **Ramp winners:** ProTex1 (520%), Seasonal Best Sellers (1,827% prospecting), Hi-Vis existing (452%), Entire Catalogue DPA (442%). WK35 already shows this working (389% on the month's highest spend).
3. **Decide awareness:** keep for the B2B/brand play or trim for pure ROAS — Stuart's call, costed above.
4. **Fill the offer slot:** LY ran clearance through late August into September; that slot is empty and is a chunk of the YoY purchase gap (-25%).
5. **Agree per-layer targets:** 5x blended and beating LY revenue pull against each other at current budget. Suggest: catalogue/category 4.5–5x+, prospecting 3x+, awareness judged on reach/CPM, with blended MER vs Shopify (10.6x in August) as the health check.
