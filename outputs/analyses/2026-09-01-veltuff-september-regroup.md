## 4. UGC — the honest answer (revised on Meta's own numbers)

Stuart's scepticism is fair. On Meta's attribution:

- **Lifetime (Apr–Sep): 4,255 DKK (~£470) spend, 236% ROAS, 20 purchases** — below the ~370% account average. July: 3,296 DKK at 259%. August: 959 DKK at 160% (4 purchases), because Advantage+ throttled delivery after July's soft results — 10 of 12 UGC ads got under 50 DKK all month.
- The engagement layer is genuinely strong (Motion: thumbstop 17% vs 5.3% account average; CTR ~3% vs 1.7%) — people stop and click, but purchases haven't followed at the same rate.
- The wider context: the two creative-testing prospecting ad sets UGC lives in ("Prospecting | Creatives" 75%, "Prospecting | Video Creatives" 126%) are the worst performers in the account. UGC is not the outlier there; the whole creative-test layer underperformed in August.
- **Recommendation:** don't defend it — agree with him and put a decision on the table: either a properly budgeted two-week test (dedicated ad set, ~150–200 DKK/day) to get a definitive verdict, or park UGC for prospecting and keep the budget in catalogue + category campaigns. Note the planned UGC shoot is still blocked by the Trade Holster 32" waist stockout (30 Jul call).

## 5. Awareness — current contribution

- August: 3,873 DKK (7.6% of spend, ~£425/month) at ~5.4 DKK CPM, reaching ~125K people/week — roughly 39% of all impressions served.
- Zero direct purchases (reach objective — it will never show ROAS). Supporting signals are positive: returning customer rate 36.3% for August (+8% YoY), conversion rate +23%.
- It underpins the B2B awareness play Stuart himself is driving (11 Aug call). But it drags blended ROAS mechanically — see the maths below. Present as a strategy choice with both options costed.

## 6. Tactics to drag ROAS to 5x

The pruning maths alone gets most of the way there:

- August blended: 368%.
- Remove the two weak creative-test prospecting sets (8,743 DKK at ~90% blended): remaining account ran at **425%**.
- Remove awareness (3,873 DKK, no direct revenue): **469%** on the sales layer alone.

So the plan:
1. **Prune:** cut or rebuild "Prospecting | Creatives" and "Prospecting | Video Creatives" — 8.7K DKK (~£960) at sub-130% in August is the single biggest ROAS lever.
2. **Ramp winners:** ProTex1 (520%), Seasonal Best Sellers (1,827% prospecting), Hi-Vis existing (452%), Entire Catalogue DPA (442%). WK35 already shows this working (389% on the month's highest spend).
3. **Decide awareness:** keep for the B2B/brand play or trim for pure ROAS — Stuart's call, costed above.
4. **Fill the offer slot:** LY ran clearance through late August into September; that slot is empty and is a chunk of the YoY purchase gap (-25%).
5. **Agree per-layer targets:** 5x blended and beating LY revenue pull against each other at current budget. Suggest: catalogue/category 4.5–5x+, prospecting 3x+, awareness judged on reach/CPM, with blended MER vs Shopify (10.6x in August) as the health check.
