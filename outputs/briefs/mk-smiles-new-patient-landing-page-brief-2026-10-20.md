## Target Audience
- **Who:** adults 28 to 45 and young families living within 3 to 5 miles of Brooklands (MK10): Brooklands, Broughton, Broughton Gate, Atterbury, Middleton, Milton Keynes Village, Oakgrove, Monkston, Kingston, Willen. Degree-educated, working, many moved into new-build homes in the last few years. The most diverse catchment in the Kana group (59% White, 64% UK-born).
- **Pain point:** they've moved to the area and don't have a dentist, or they've been putting it off because appointments clash with work and the school run. Some are nervous after a bad experience.
- **Desire:** one local practice for the whole family, appointments that fit around work (evenings, Saturdays), a dentist who explains things and doesn't rush or upsell.
- **Objections:** "Will I get an appointment soon?", "What does it actually cost?", "Will they push treatment on me?", "I'm nervous", "Is it worth going private?"

## Offer
- **Primary offer:** New Patient Assessment. **Price to confirm before build:** the site's prices page shows two fee guides that conflict: "New Patient Assessment – Adult £50.00" in one, "New Patient Check-up (including 2 routine x-rays) £76.70" in the other. Child assessment £25, or free for children when a parent attends at the same time (second guide).
- **Value proposition:** a private practice inside Brooklands Health Centre with late evenings Monday and Wednesday (to 7pm) and Saturdays 8:45am to 4:30pm. The nearest direct competitor (Oakgrove) closes at weekends. Family care under one roof, rated 4.8 from 295 Google reviews.
- **Urgency/scarcity:** none. No live offer has been agreed, so no "limited spaces" or deadline language.

### Problem / Agitation
New to the area, or overdue a check-up? Finding a dentist who can see you around work and the school run is harder than it should be. Weekday-only appointments mean taking time off, and if you've had a rushed or uncomfortable visit before, it's easy to keep putting it off.

### Solution
- **Appointments that fit your week:** late evenings Monday and Wednesday, and Saturdays until 4:30pm.
- **One practice for the family:** adults and children seen together; children's exams free when a parent attends at the same time (confirm still current).
- **Time to explain:** a thorough first assessment with x-rays and a clear plan, and nothing goes ahead without your say-so.
- **Support for nervous patients:** a calm, unhurried approach. Do not use "pain-free" or sedation language here.

### How It Works
1. **Book your assessment:** fill in the form and the team will call you to arrange a time, including evenings and Saturdays.
2. **Your first visit:** a full check-up with x-rays at Brooklands Health Centre, with time to ask questions.
3. **Your plan:** a clear explanation of anything you need, with prices before anything goes ahead.

## Technical Notes
- Mobile-first design; sticky header with the Book Now button on mobile
- Page load target: < 3 seconds. Use the web-size photos (1,800px max), not the 6,000px originals
- Tracking: Meta Pixel, with View Content on page load (the dental lead proxy) and Lead on form submit. Keep the existing General campaign's pixel and events
- No phone numbers, call buttons or WhatsApp on the landing page. The form is the only CTA
- Thank-you page: create new. Confirmation message, what happens next, and an online booking link: booking.uk.hsone.app/soe/new/MK%20Smiles?pid=UKMKM02 (from the Aug LP brief; check it's live)
- Form submission: Boxly (Kana's lead CRM), with UTM parameters passed through so Meta leads are tagged. Speed-to-lead matters: the 1 Oct call found an 11-hour average first response across Kana, so flag to Kana that these leads need a same-day call
