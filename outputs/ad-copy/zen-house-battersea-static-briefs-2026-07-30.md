# Creative Briefs: Zen House Dental Battersea — New Statics
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Scope:** 12 new static creatives (10 briefs; two are multi-card/multi-execution) to complete the persona-led ad sets for the Battersea launch
**Formats:** Every static in 1080×1080 (feed) and 1080×1350 (4:5) unless stated. Carousels 1080×1080 per card.
**Design language:** Match the existing Zen House Battersea set in Figma — serif headline with italic emphasis on the second phrase, warm cream/beige palette, ZEN HOUSE DENTAL logotype top, pill CTA bar bottom.
**File naming:** `ZH-BAT_[SegmentCode]_[Concept]_[SQ|4x5|C01..]` e.g. `ZH-BAT_S3_Price-Certainty_SQ`

**Global compliance rules (apply to every brief):**
- No second-person framing that implies knowledge of the viewer's teeth, health, fear or relationship status (Meta personal attributes).
- No "perfect", "flawless", "dream smile", "painless", "guaranteed".
- No em dashes in ad copy.
- Any price shown must match the landing page it clicks to, and Battersea pricing must be confirmed by the client before launch (report assumption 12.1).
- "Free consultation" may only appear on Invisalign creatives until the £30 makeover consultation conflict is resolved.

## Campaign: ZH BAT P1 General — ad set S3 Newly-Landed

### 1. Price Certainty — `ZH-BAT_S3_Price-Certainty`
- **Angle:** Assumed cost is this segment's biggest objection. Publish the price inside the creative so the objection never forms.
- **Headline:** £50 check-ups. *No surprises.*
- **Sub-line:** A full examination including x-rays, at our new practice on Northcote Road.
- **CTA bar:** Book your check-up
- **Visual:** The Battersea shopfront on Northcote Road, shot straight-on with recognisable street context (neighbouring shopfronts, planting, pavement). Daylight. The building is the hook for this persona, not people or teeth.
- **Compliance/confirm:** £50 price and x-ray inclusion confirmed for Battersea before launch.

### 2. Availability — `ZH-BAT_S3_Availability`
- **Angle:** Evenings and Saturdays is the underexploited competitive gap in the catchment (several rivals are weekday-only). Wandsworth is ~80% employed and time-poor.
- **Headline:** Evenings and *Saturdays.*
- **Sub-line:** Dental appointments that fit around work and the school run. Now open on Northcote Road.
- **CTA bar:** Book online
- **Visual:** Warm interior reception shot at dusk, lights on, or the shopfront lit in early evening. Communicates "open when you're free".
- **Compliance/confirm:** BLOCKED until Battersea opening hours are confirmed — none are published anywhere. Do not build final artwork until hours are agreed.

### 3. Zen Plan Carousel — `ZH-BAT_S3_Zen-Plan_C01–C04`
- **Angle:** Converts a one-off visit into a relationship; monthly framing ("cheaper than my gym") is the System 2 justification for this persona.
- **Card 1 (hook):** Headline: One plan. *Everything handled.* Sub: Dental membership from £18.90 a month.
- **Card 2 (Plan A):** Zen Plan A, £18.90/month. 2 check-ups, 2 hygiene visits, x-rays as needed, 10% off treatments.
- **Card 3 (Plan B):** Zen Plan B, £28.90/month. As Plan A with 4 hygiene visits a year.
- **Card 4 (close):** Headline: Join in *two minutes.* Sub: Northcote Road, Battersea. CTA: Sign up online.
- **Visual:** Clean typographic cards on the cream palette, one accent photograph per card (interior detail, not clinical). Price is the hero element.
- **Compliance/confirm:** Plan pricing and inclusions confirmed for Battersea. The Denplan signup link is currently hard-coded to the Banstead postcode (SM7) — must be fixed before this runs or signups route to Surrey.

### 4. Review Proof — `ZH-BAT_S3_Review-Proof`
- **Angle:** Proof transfer. The 580-review base is a brand fact, not a Banstead fact. This is the single highest-leverage message in the launch.
- **Headline:** Rated 5.0 from *580+ Google reviews.*
- **Sub-line:** Zen House Dental. Now open on Northcote Road, Battersea.
- **CTA bar:** Book your check-up
- **Visual:** Five gold stars rendered in the brand style above the headline. Background: soft interior or signage photograph. No stock review widgets.
- **Compliance/confirm:** Re-verify the live review count on the day artwork is finalised; the figure in the ad must be true at time of running. Say "580+" only while the count supports it.

## Campaign: ZH BAT P2 Invisalign — ad set S2 Junction-Climber

### 5. Monthly Price Carousel — `ZH-BAT_S2_Monthly-Price_C01–C04`
- **Angle:** This persona will not enquire without a price range. Price transparency inside the creative wins the click.
- **Card 1 (hook):** Headline: Invisalign, from *£[X] a month.* Sub: 0% finance for up to 12 months.
- **Cards 2–4:** One card per Invisalign tier with treatment scope, total price and the monthly figure at 0% over 12 months.
- **Final card:** Free consultation with a 3D scan. CTA: Book a free consultation.
- **Visual:** Typographic price cards, aligner product photography or the existing macro smile assets as secondary texture. Monthly figure is the hero.
- **Compliance/confirm:** BLOCKED until pricing is reconciled — the fees page floor (£1,900) does not exist as a purchasable tier on the treatment page (£3,000/£3,500/£4,000). Every figure must match the landing page. Finance figures must be accurate to the Tabeo terms (representative example if required).

### 6. Scan Preview — `ZH-BAT_S2_Scan-Preview`
- **Angle:** Certainty. The iTero outcome preview compresses the distance between interest and appointment: known result, known cost, known finish date.
- **Headline:** See your result *before you start.*
- **Sub-line:** A free consultation with a 3D scan shows your finished smile before you commit to anything.
- **CTA bar:** Book a free consultation
- **Visual:** A still of the iTero scan interface on the in-practice screen, clinician's hand gesturing at it, patient softly out of focus. Genuine UI, not a mock-up.
- **Compliance/confirm:** "Free" is genuine for Invisalign consultations; keep this creative in the Invisalign campaign only.

## Campaign: ZH BAT P2 Invisalign — ad set S2 Engaged

### 7. Timeline Maths — `ZH-BAT_S2_Engaged-Timeline`
- **Angle:** The engaged buyer has a fixed date and a 9–18 month lead. State the timeline as a fact; never address the viewer's relationship status directly (personal attributes policy).
- **Headline:** Most brides start Invisalign *a year out.*
- **Sub-line:** Treatment typically takes 6 to 12 months. A free 3D scan shows the finish line before you begin.
- **CTA bar:** Book a free consultation
- **Visual:** Flat-lay or detail shot that reads "wedding planning": invitation suite, calendar, ring box, in the brand palette. No countdown clocks, no urgency devices.
- **Compliance/confirm:** Treatment duration claim must reflect what the clinicians will stand behind; confirm the typical range.

### 8. Photo Day — `ZH-BAT_S2_Engaged-Photo-Day`
- **Angle:** A wedding is a day of photographs. Sell the feeling of not thinking about it. Restraint, not transformation.
- **Headline:** A day of photographs. *Not a single retake.*
- **Sub-line:** Invisalign and composite bonding, planned digitally at our Northcote Road practice.
- **CTA bar:** Book a free consultation
- **Visual:** Candid, unposed couple mid-laugh, editorial not stocky. Natural light. No teeth macro. The moment is the message.
- **Compliance/confirm:** Imagery must be licensed or consented; no real patient imagery without documented paid-media consent.

## Campaign: ZH BAT P2 Makeover — ad set S1 Reclaimer

### 10. Peer Moment — `ZH-BAT_S1_Peer-Moment`
- **Angle:** The report's System 1 trigger for this persona: a woman her age, unposed, mid-laugh, clearly not thinking about her teeth. Permission and restraint together. The clinic appears second, not first.
- **Headline:** It will look like *you.*
- **Sub-line:** Composite bonding and Invisalign, designed digitally with a named clinician, so the result is yours and nobody else's.
- **CTA bar:** Book a consultation *(not "free" — the makeover consultation is currently £30)*
- **Visual:** Woman aged 40–50, no filter, genuine laugh, natural setting that reads South West London (common, café terrace, Victorian terrace doorway). Golden hour. Absolutely no clinical environment, no side-by-side, no teeth macro.
- **Compliance/confirm:** Model or consented patient only. Avoid any visual that implies the "before" was inadequate (negative self-perception rule).

## Campaign: ZH BAT P1 General — ad set S4 Guarded-Patient (hold until complete)

### 11. Calm Set — `ZH-BAT_S4_Calm_01–03`
Three executions, one message each. Tone is the proposition: gentle, slow, no exclamation marks, no urgency. Visual language closer to a wellness brand than a dental brand. **Never** name the viewer's fear, absence or dental state ("Nervous?", "Been a while?" are both policy failures and psychologically wrong).

- **01 — The conversation.** Headline: The first visit is *just a conversation.* Sub: A chat, a look, and a written plan to take away. Nothing else happens on the day. Visual: clinician's face, warm, unhurried, calm room. No instruments, no chair, no gloves, no mouths.
- **02 — Consent.** Headline: Nothing happens *without your say so.* Sub: You set the pace at every appointment. Visual: the practice interior, soft light, empty and calm.
- **03 — No judgement.** Headline: No lectures. *No judgement.* Sub: However long it's been, you'll be treated like a person, not a project. Visual: reception welcome moment, faces not procedures.
- **CTA bar (all three):** Book a conversation
- **Compliance/confirm:** None beyond the global rules, but hold the whole ad set until all executions exist; do not mix these with cosmetic creative.

### 12. Nervous-Patient Review Quote — `ZH-BAT_S4_Review-Quote`
- **Angle:** Belonging. Proof that people who felt the same came here and were fine.
- **Headline:** A real patient review, verbatim, from the Zen House Google corpus. **[QUOTE TO BE SELECTED FROM REAL REVIEWS — do not draft or paraphrase one. Requires the reviewer's documented consent for paid media use.]**
- **Sub-line:** One of 580+ five star Google reviews.
- **CTA bar:** Book a conversation
- **Visual:** Typographic quote card in the brand serif, five stars, no photography needed.
- **Compliance/confirm:** Consent from the reviewer; add "Individual results may differ" if the quote references a treatment outcome.
