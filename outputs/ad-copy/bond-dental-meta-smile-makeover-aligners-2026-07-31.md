## Campaign structure

```
Campaign: BD | Smile Makeover & Aligners | Leads          (Advantage campaign budget)
├── Ad set 1: Prospecting — Boardroom Smile               (~35% budget)
├── Ad set 2: Prospecting — Milestone Investor            (~30% budget)
├── Ad set 3: Prospecting — Discerning Perfectionist      (~10% budget, always-on brand presence)
└── Ad set 4: Retargeting — Warm (30–90d)                 (~25% budget)
```

Budget posture follows the Audience Intelligence Report tiering: Tier 1 (Camera-Ready Professional = Boardroom Smile; Milestone Investor) carries ~65% of prospecting; the Quiet Perfectionist (Tier 3) is capped at ~10% as an always-on premium presence in W1/W11/W14 postcodes and **excluded from price-led messaging**.

- **Placements:** Advantage+. Upload both formats per ad (1:1 feed + 9:16 story).
- **Targeting:** broad 25–54 for prospecting; creative self-selects. Per the report: layer light interest stacks only for learning-phase efficiency (Invisalign, cosmetic dentistry, professional development). Milestone ad set adds life-event signals: newly engaged, birthday in 30–90 days, wedding-planning interests.
- **Retargeting:** website visitors 90d + IG/FB engagers 90d, excluding leads 180d. Exclude all warm audiences from prospecting.
- **Lookalike seeds (Phase 2, per report):** completed Invisalign/cosmetic patients from CRM → whitening purchasers → consultation bookers.
- **Seasonality (Milestone):** engagement season Dec–Feb, pre-summer wedding run-up Jan–Apr, graduation May–Jul.

## Testing recommendations

- **Test variable:** angle within each ad set (pain-agitation vs comparison vs bundle-maths). One variable at a time.
- **Split:** equal delivery, kill the bottom half after 3–5 days at meaningful spend, scale winners.
- **Key metric:** CPL against booked £95 consultations in GHL (channel: Paid Social), then show-rate and plan acceptance (the report's Phase 1 benchmarks). Watch qualified-lead rate, not raw CPL.
- **Timing:** monthly-cost framing lands hardest in the first week of the month (payday effect, per report).
- **Story safe zones:** headlines and CTAs sit inside the Reels-safe area on the 9:16 exports; no text overlays in Ads Manager.

## Not in this campaign
- Second Act persona (implants, full arch): Campaign 2 — Implants. (Note: this persona was added by the creative team; it is not in the Audience Intelligence Report's five segments.)
- NHS Exile + The Anxious Returner (new patients, membership, sedation): Campaign 3 — New Patients. Report playbook for the Anxious Returner: late-evening mobile Reels/Stories placements, no urgency mechanics ever, active comment moderation (peer reassurance lifts conversion), sedation-led messaging as the defensible territory.
- Emergency (BD-39/40): always-on utility, small budget. Same-day availability is the highest-intent entry point for NHS-displaced patients.
