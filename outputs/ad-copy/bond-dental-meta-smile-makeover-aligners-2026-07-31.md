## Testing recommendations

- **Test variable:** angle within each ad set (pain-agitation vs comparison vs price-led). Keep creative constant per ad while testing text, or vice versa, never both at once.
- **Split:** launch all variants with equal delivery, kill the bottom half after 3–5 days at meaningful spend, scale winners.
- **Key metric:** CPL against booked £95 consultations in GHL (channel: Paid Social). Watch qualified-lead rate, not just raw CPL, since Boardroom and Perfectionist leads should convert to treatment plans at higher values.
- **Story safe zones:** headlines and CTAs already sit inside the Reels-safe area on the 9:16 exports; do not add text overlays in Ads Manager.
