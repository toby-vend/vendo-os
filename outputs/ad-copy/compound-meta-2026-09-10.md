## RiskSave rules applied

- **No timing claims on the pension search** — Never say the search or transfer takes a minute, is instant, quick, or takes seconds. Approved wording is 'One search, one app, easy'. Describing the app is fine; describing how long the full process takes is not. _(Source: Becki, 04/09: 'One search about a minute is misleading, it does not make it sufficiently clear that the full process will take longer.'. Applies to: B2C-03, B2C-04, and any future find-and-combine copy)_
- **No projections, returns or growth figures** — Do not state or imply a pot value at retirement, a rate of return, or what someone could have if they start earlier. Any illustration needs the growth assumption disclosed on the asset, which does not fit the format. _(Source: Becki, 04/09 (h): 'There is no rate of return provided on the 22 vs 40 projection... we cannot approve this given how little information it provides customers.'. Applies to: All B2C. 22 vs 40 was withdrawn on this basis)_
- **Do not presume personal circumstances or guarantee outcomes** — No 'you could have', 'you will be', 'just £20 a week and you are sorted'. Soften to what the product does, not what the customer's life looks like. _(Source: Becki, 04/09 (i): 'We cannot presume to know anyone's personal circumstances and we do not want to guarantee, the language needs to be softened.'. Applies to: All B2C. 20 A Week was withdrawn on this basis)_

'Free at any client count' and 'free for your practice' are approved.
