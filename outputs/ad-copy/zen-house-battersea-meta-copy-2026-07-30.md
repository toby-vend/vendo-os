# Meta Ad Copy: Zen House Dental Battersea
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Structure:** One copy set per ad set (persona-led). The copy runs across all 3–5 creatives in that ad set; where a creative needs its own line, it is noted.
**Style:** Follows the Vendo Creative System (pain amplified before solution, first line written for Meta's transcript/copy reading) within the compliance rules from the Battersea audience report: no second-person framing that implies knowledge of the viewer's teeth, health, fear or relationship status. Pain is carried by scenario and narrative, never by "you".

**Placeholders that must be resolved before launch (do not load ads containing them):**
- `£[X]` — Invisalign monthly finance figure (blocked by the site's pricing conflict)
- £50 exam, Zen Plan prices, evenings/Saturdays — confirm for Battersea
- "580+ reviews" — re-verify count at load
- "Europe's Top 1% Invisalign provider" / "Diamond Apex" — NOT used below; add only once the client provides substantiation in writing

## Load notes

1. **Consultation CTAs differ by campaign.** "Free consultation" appears only in Invisalign copy. Makeover copy says "book a consultation" because the smile makeover consultation is currently £30 and non-refundable on the site. If the client makes it free, the copy can be upgraded.
2. **"Typically 6 to 12 months"** appears in three copy sets and must be signed off by the clinicians before launch.
3. **The S4 set does not mention price or treatments.** That is deliberate. Adding offers to this ad set breaks the tone that makes it work.
4. **Transcript-first rule:** the first line of each long text is the targeting signal. Do not let Ads Manager truncation push a generic line to the top by reordering.
5. All prices must match the Battersea landing page on the day of load, per the ASA pricing rule and the site's known pricing conflicts.
