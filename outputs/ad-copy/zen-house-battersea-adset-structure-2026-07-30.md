# Ad Set Structure Guide: Zen House Dental Battersea
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Scope:** Full Meta Ads build structure for the Battersea launch. Three campaigns, seven ad sets, persona-led. Companion documents: the static creative briefs (2026-07-30) and the Meta ad copy document (2026-07-30). Personas are drawn from the Battersea Audience Intelligence Report (July 2026).

---

## 1. Structural Principles

1. **One persona per ad set.** The ad set is the persona unit. Targeting, tone and creative all serve one buyer psychology. Never mix personas in an ad set; it blurs the targeting signal and makes results unreadable.
2. **3–5 creatives per ad set**, each a *different angle* on the same persona. Image variants of the same design count as one creative.
3. **Creative does the targeting.** Broad audiences, no interest stacking, no health-adjacent detailed targeting (removed by Meta and a rejection risk). The first line of copy and the headline on the static tell the algorithm who to find.
4. **Proof transfer is the launch strategy.** The 580+ review base, award history and accreditations are brand facts, not Banstead facts. They appear across every ad set.
5. **Sequencing:** weight budget to the General campaign for weeks 1–6 (approx. 70/20/10 General/Invisalign/Makeover), shifting to cosmetic-majority from week 5–6 as Battersea reviews build. Cosmetic-first into a one-review listing loses the comparison against Glow Dental (188 reviews, same street).
6. **Winners are untouchable.** Never switch off an ad inside an ad set hitting KPI. New ads for a winning persona go into a new ad set.
7. **Measurement:** cost per lead is diagnostic only. The real metrics are cost per attended consultation and cost per treatment start. Report consultation attendance rate weekly from week 3.

## 2. Naming Convention

```
Campaign:  ZH BAT P[phase] [function]        e.g. ZH BAT P2 Invisalign
Ad set:    [campaign] | [segment] [persona]  e.g. ZH BAT P2 Invisalign | S2 Junction-Climber
Ad:        ZH BAT P2 | S2 | [Concept] | IMG-03 | 4x5
```

Segment codes: S1 Reclaimer, S2 Junction Climber, S3 Newly Landed, S4 Guarded Patient, S5 Household Gatekeeper, RT retargeting. Never name audiences or ad sets after health conditions or perceived flaws (Meta reviews audience names).

## 3. Campaign 1: ZH BAT P1 General

**Objective:** Leads (booked exams and plan signups). Battersea patient volume and review velocity. This campaign funds and de-risks everything else.

### Ad set: `ZH BAT P1 General | S3 Newly-Landed`

**Persona — The Newly Landed.** Moved to SW11 within the last two years and never re-registered with a dentist. Any age 25–55, both catchment populations. Not afraid of the dentist; blocked by friction. Believes NHS access is effectively gone and assumes private costs an unknowable amount. Wants *a dentist*, not a treatment; registering is part of properly living somewhere. The single thing to say: **"A dentist on Northcote Road. £50 check-ups. Evenings and Saturdays."**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Geography | 1–2 mile radius of SW11 6QZ. Do not extend; this persona converts on proximity |
| Age / gender | 25–55, all |
| Audience | Broad + Advantage+; "recently moved" life event as anchor audience |
| Placements | Advantage+ (statics carry feed; 4:5 covers stories) |
| Budget role | ~70% of campaign in weeks 1–6 |
| KPI | Cost per booked exam |

**Creatives (5):** Now-Open signage static (existing) · Price-Certainty shopfront (£50) · Availability (evenings/Saturdays) · Zen Plan carousel · Review-Proof (580+).

### Ad set: `ZH BAT P1 General | S4 Guarded-Patient` — hold until creative complete

**Persona — The Guarded Patient.** Carries real dental anxiety rooted in an old bad experience; avoidance measured in years, often with shame layered on top. Age 28–70, cuts across both populations. Not choosing a practice; choosing a person who will not judge them. The purchase is the ability to walk in without adrenaline. Anxiety is the dominant emotional theme in this catchment's review corpus and no competitor owns the position. The single thing to say: **"Come in, have a conversation, and we will not do anything you have not agreed to."**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Geography | 3–5 mile radius; this persona travels for the right practice |
| Age / gender | 30–65, all. Do not narrow; the defining trait is not demographic |
| Audience | Broad. Not targetable via interests and must not be attempted; the creative self-selects |
| Placements | Feed weighted; this persona needs time with the message, not a scroll-stopper |
| Budget role | ~20% as brand/nurture; real activation comes with clinician video in Phase 3 |
| KPI | Cost per lead, lead quality, video view audience growth |

**Creatives (4, all to create):** Calm 01 conversation · Calm 02 consent · Calm 03 no judgement · Review-Quote (real, consented).
**Rule:** no prices, no treatments, no cosmetic creative in this ad set. Exclude its audience from cosmetic retargeting to avoid tonal collision.

## 4. Campaign 2: ZH BAT P2 Invisalign

**Objective:** Leads (free consultations with 3D scan). Starts week 1 at low weight, takes the majority of budget from week 5–6.

### Ad set: `ZH BAT P2 Invisalign | S2 Junction-Climber`

**Persona — The Junction Climber.** 25–35, renting around Clapham Junction, Battersea Rise and Lavender Hill. Earns well, no dependants, treats appearance as professional competence. Has considered Invisalign at least twice, probably had a consultation elsewhere and stalled. Product-aware: the barrier is unknowns (price, duration, visibility at work), not knowledge. Decides fast once the unknowns are answered; will not enquire without a price range. The single thing to say: **"See the finished result before you start, and know exactly what it costs per month."**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Geography | 2–3 mile radius weighted north (Junction, Battersea Rise, Lavender Hill) + SW4, SW12, SW18, SW8 |
| Age / gender | 25–35, all. 18+ enforced (cosmetic rule) |
| Audience | Broad + "newly employed" life event layer; lookalike later from Banstead Invisalign under-35 seed |
| Placements | Reels/Stories weighted; verticals mandatory |
| KPI | Cost per consultation booked |

**Creatives (5):** Discreet by Design (existing, 2 best variants) · Straight Teeth Discreetly (existing) · Every Aligner + retainers (existing; confirm offer) · Monthly-Price carousel (create; blocked on pricing) · Scan-Preview (create).

### Ad set: `ZH BAT P2 Invisalign | S2 Engaged`

**Persona — the engaged sub-segment of the Junction Climber.** Same person, plus a date in the diary 9–18 months out. The deadline converts "one day" into a plan; the strongest single receptivity moment in the segment. Never address relationship status in second person (Meta personal attributes); state timeline facts instead.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Geography / age | As Junction-Climber |
| Audience | "Recently engaged" life event |
| KPI | Cost per consultation booked |

**Creatives (3):** Saving "I do" deseasonalised (existing, copy tweak) · Timeline-Maths (create) · Photo-Day (create).

### Ad set: `ZH BAT P2 Invisalign | RT`

Retargets Invisalign page visitors, lead-form abandoners and 75% video viewers (last 30 days). Objection: cost and commitment. **Creatives (2–3):** Finance-Reassurance (create) · Scan-Preview reuse · Review-Proof reuse. KPI: consultation-to-attendance rate.

## 5. Campaign 3: ZH BAT P2 Makeover

**Objective:** Leads (bonding and smile makeover consultations). Same phasing as Invisalign.

### Ad set: `ZH BAT P2 Makeover | S1 Reclaimer`

**Persona — The Reclaimer.** Woman 36–50 between the commons; family, Victorian terrace, part-time professional or self-employed. Has managed everyone else's healthcare for a decade while her own teeth stayed "fine". Trigger is visual: a photo or video of herself at a family event. Solution-aware but doesn't know who to trust; deeply suspicious of anything that looks "done". Would rather do nothing than look artificial; vanity-framing repels her. The proposition is permission plus restraint. Slow decision cycle; do not expect first-month attribution. The single thing to say: **"It will look like you, and you are allowed to want it."**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Geography | 1.5–2 mile radius weighted south (Between the Commons, Wandsworth Common, Balham) |
| Age / gender | Women 36–50 primary. 18+ enforced |
| Audience | Broad + lifestyle proxies only if needed (premium grocery, pilates, interiors); no dental interests |
| Placements | Feed weighted; unhurried formats |
| Sequencing | Run 2–3 weeks on engagement/video views before switching to leads; this persona does not convert cold |
| KPI | Cost per consultation booked (judge from week 4+) |

**Creatives (5):** The Art of Invisible Repair (existing) · Day One to Day Done (existing) · Go-To Clinic for Composite Bonding + 580-review proof line (existing, amend) · Clear Choice for Smile Makeovers (existing) · Peer-Moment (create).

### Ad set: `ZH BAT P2 Makeover | RT`

Retargets bonding/makeover page visitors and S1 engagers. The **only** place before/after imagery may appear: 18+, neutral captioning, "Results may vary" disclaimer on the artwork, no problem framing. **Creatives (4):** Cracks, Chips, Gaps before/after variants (existing, add disclaimer). KPI: consultation-to-attendance rate.

## 6. Exclusions Matrix (set on every prospecting ad set)

| Exclusion | Applies to |
|---|---|
| Existing patient list (live sync) | All |
| Consultation bookers, last 60 days | All |
| Banstead catchment postcodes | All (protects the Surrey practice and attribution) |
| Under-18s | All cosmetic ad sets (hard rule) |
| S4 Guarded audience | Both cosmetic campaigns' retargeting (tonal collision) |
| Each campaign's converters | The other two campaigns' retargeting |

## 7. Budget Sequencing

| Weeks | General | Invisalign | Makeover | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | 70% | 20% | 10% | Makeover in engagement mode, not leads |
| 5–8 | 40% | 35% | 25% | Cosmetic switches to leads as reviews build |
| 9+ | 25% | 45% | 30% | Review winners; feed Battersea seed into lookalikes from week 8 |

Minimum ~£10–15/day per active ad set so everything exits learning. ABO during testing; consider CBO per campaign once each has a stable winner.

## 8. Future Ad Set (Phase 3, from ~week 12)

**`ZH BAT P3 Family | S5 Household-Gatekeeper`** — mother 34–48 between the commons registering a whole household; highest lifetime value and the reason the location was bought. Chooses on school-gate recommendation; the family-founded story is the unlock. Blocked today by asset readiness (founder film, children-in-practice content with consent, local parent testimonials). The single thing to say: **"One practice for the whole family, run by a family, five minutes from your door."**

2. Battersea landing page live with local team, address, Battersea phone number
3. Pixel verified firing + conversion event defined (last fire recorded 2026-03-17)
4. Pricing reconciled (Invisalign tiers, bonding per-tooth vs packages) and confirmed for Battersea
5. Battersea opening hours confirmed
6. Denplan link repointed from SM7
7. Consent verified for any patient imagery or review quotes
8. Substantiation on file before "Top 1% in Europe" or award claims run
