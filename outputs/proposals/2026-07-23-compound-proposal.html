Where Compound is

Compound is building the modern workplace pension — an app that makes pensions simple for the people who hold them, the employers who provide them, and the accountants and advisors who manage them. You're tackling a genuinely large problem (the UK's ~£50 billion in lost pensions) with a product designed to be understood in plain terms.

That gives you three distinct audiences, each of which responds to different messaging and lives on different channels:

Employers

Businesses choosing or switching their workplace pension provider. Considered, multi-stakeholder decision.

Individuals

People with lost or scattered pension pots. Emotional, curiosity-driven hook — "find your lost pensions in 60 seconds."

Accountants & advisors

Professionals managing pensions for multiple clients. A partner channel that multiplies every win.

Fintech advertising lives or dies on creative quality and message clarity. Stock imagery and generic "sort your pension" ads don't cut through — real founders, real product UI, and plain-English explanations do. That's the engine we build for you.

Our recommended approach

Paid Social — Meta

  • Video-led campaigns built from your content shoot — founder-to-camera, product demos, explainer cuts
  • Static ads designed in-house to test hooks, offers and audiences quickly
  • Separate campaign structures per audience (individuals vs employers/advisors)
  • Full pixel, Conversions API and event tracking setup

Paid Search — Google

  • Capture high-intent demand: lost pension search, pension consolidation, workplace pension provider comparisons
  • Brand protection so competitors can't sit on your name
  • Conversion tracking wired to the actions that matter (app installs, sign-ups, demo requests)
  • Continuous search-term mining and negative keyword management

The two channels compound each other: Meta builds awareness and demand; Google captures it when people go looking. Running both under one roof means one strategy, one feedback loop, one report.

A note on compliance: financial promotions on Meta and Google require FCA-authorised advertiser verification. As Compound is FCA-regulated this is straightforward — we handle the verification process and build ads to financial-promotion standards (clear risk wording, no misleading claims) as standard.

The creative engine

Most agencies ask you for assets. We come and make them. Your package includes:

  • Upfront content shoot — a professional videography day at the start of the engagement. We plan the shot list around the ad concepts we want to run: founder pieces, product walk-throughs, testimonial-style content and hook variations.
  • A fresh shoot every 6 months — creative fatigues; audiences stop responding to ads they've seen. A refresh shoot each cycle keeps the account fed with new video without you lifting a finger.
  • Static ad design, ongoing — our in-house design team produces static creative throughout: new hooks, seasonal angles, audience-specific messages. Statics let us test fast between shoots.

Everything we shoot is yours — the footage works across your website, socials and sales materials, not just the ad account.

What ongoing management looks like

Every week

  • Budget pacing, bid and audience optimisation
  • Creative performance review — scale winners, kill losers
  • Search-term and placement hygiene

Every month

  • Performance report in plain English
  • Strategy call — results, learnings, next tests
  • New creative briefs into design and the next shoot plan

Growing with you: your future marketing hire

As Compound scales, you'll likely bring marketing in-house. We see that as a milestone, not a threat. When you're ready to hire, we'll help you onboard and train your new marketing team member — walking them through the account structures, creative learnings and reporting we've built, so they start from everything we've learned rather than from zero. We can then flex into a supporting role: shoots, creative production and specialist channel management alongside your in-house lead.
