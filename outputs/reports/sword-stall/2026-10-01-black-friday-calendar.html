How each channel runs it

One plan across ads, site and inbox

Meta

  • A dedicated Black Friday campaign, scheduled 19–30 Nov, so sale ads switch off on their own
  • Two audiences: new customers, and people who already know you
  • Evergreen Scaling campaign keeps running; testing pauses for the fortnight
  • Every ad built and approved before 16 Nov

Google

  • Sale prices flow into Shopping automatically from the site
  • Merchant Center promotions submitted early November
  • Search ads carry the deals as headlines and promotion assets
  • Budgets lifted on the peak days

Website

  • One Black Friday collection page all ads point to
  • Homepage banner swaps to the day's spotlight deal
  • Early-access sign-up, then a countdown to Cyber Monday

Email and SMS

Your team. We'll match ad timings to your sends.

  • Early-access list built 2–18 Nov
  • One send per spotlight deal, two on Black Friday
  • Final reminder on Cyber Monday

To lock it in

What we need from you

  1. Sale dates. Happy with early access on 19 Nov and a 30 Nov finish?
  2. Margin at deal price. Quick check on the £39 pistol and £149 shield.
  3. Stock depth. Units on each deal line, so we know what can carry spend.
  4. How discounts show. Sale prices on the products, or codes at checkout?
  5. Christmas order-by dates. Last dates for DPD and DX, for the December ads.
  6. Email and SMS dates. Your send plan, so ads land on the same days.
