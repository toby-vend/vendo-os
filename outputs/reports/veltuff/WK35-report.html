UGC — top of funnel view Answers "where is the UGC value?"

UGC was never built to close sales on its own ad. Its job is the introduction, and Meta's last-click reporting hands the sale to whichever ad finishes it. Read left to right — this is what actually happens to a UGC viewer:

Step 1 · Stop the scroll
UGC earns the attention
Hook rate ≈3× the account average — the strongest attention numbers in the account.
Step 2 · Visit
They click through to the store
CTR 2.46% this week vs 1.81% across the sales campaigns.
Step 3 · Shop
They shop like any other visitor
9% of clickers add to cart — identical to the account average (Jul–Aug). UGC traffic is real shopping traffic, not empty engagement.
Step 4 · The close
A catalogue or retargeting ad finishes the sale — and takes the credit
That closing layer ran 372% Jul–Aug. This is why UGC looks invisible in last-click summaries.
