Interest is up, checkout is leaking. Twenty two percent more people put product in a basket than the same week last year. Four percent fewer reached checkout and twelve percent fewer finished. The ads are doing their job all the way to the basket and the loss is entirely in the last two steps. Worth checking against last year: delivery thresholds and charges, payment methods available, and anything that changed in the checkout since. Shipping charges collected are up 44% year on year, which is the first place we would look.

Actions
  • Widen the retargeting pool. Frequency of 6.25 while reaching only 11,987 people is the clearest inefficiency in the account and explains the whole year on year decline in that layer.
  • Checkout completion is the priority. Add to cart is up 22% and completion is down 12%, so the loss is after the ad has done its work. Shipping charges up 44% year on year is the first thing we would check.
  • Hold ProTex1 and Hi-Vis at current scale through their strongest season. Both are above 470% and last year they ran strongly from September to November.
  • Push Seasonal Best Sellers harder. It is returning 1,608% on £240, the highest return in the account by a distance, and the budget does not reflect that.
  • Absorb the 17% budget shortfall into the widened retargeting pool rather than into broad prospecting, where the cost per thousand impressions is up 54% year on year.
  • September offer slot still open. Last year clearance covered this window, and this year we have matched the revenue without it, so the decision is now about whether to add to a good month rather than rescue a weak one.
