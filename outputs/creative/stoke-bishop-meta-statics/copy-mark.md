# Mark: the lapsed patient | Meta copy

Client: Stoke Bishop Dental Centre, Bristol BS9. Campaign: Meta General (new patient check-ups). Persona: Mark, 35 to 55, last visit 5+ years ago. Stopped by the thought of being judged, assumes the bill grows with the gap, doesn't know what a first appointment involves (Dental Persona Bank, 01 General Dentistry). Research and sources: research.md.

Offer across all three: new patient check-up £85, which includes two small x-rays (stokebishop-dentalcentre.co.uk/costs). The price is on screen in every concept so it filters out people who won't book. Lead quality was the client's main worry on 13 Oct.

Rules for this persona:
- No sedation language. No finance figures. No student discount.
- No "free" anything that isn't on the site.
- Copy is written to "you". The client's shoot has no adult male patient, so the pictured patient is the shoot's patient model, used across all three concepts.
- Proof is volume only: 4.9 from 185 Google reviews. Named site testimonials need permission first.
- UK English, no em dashes.

Ad names follow the build standard: `<Concept> | Static | <Talent> | <Detail> | <1x1 / 9x16 / 1.91x1> | YYMMDD`. Talent is "Practice shoot" for all three.
