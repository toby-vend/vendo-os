## 2. Conflicts and checks for the client

- **Check-up price wording.** LP and live ads say "Only £75"; the fees page says "From £75". Ads use "£75" with no "from". Confirm £75 is the fixed new patient price.
- **X-rays.** LP heading and fees page say 2 X-rays included; the LP body and one live ad say "if required". Ads say "X-rays" without a number. Confirm.
- **Review proof.** LP banner says "35+ 5-Star Google Reviews". The Trustindex widget on the same page shows "GOOD", 4.5 stars, "Based on 43 reviews". No star rating or count goes on the image until the real Google figure is confirmed. Primary text quotes reviews unnamed; named use needs the reviewer's permission.
- **Phone number.** Client Bio says 01305 605353; site says 01305 782077. Not on the ads.
- **Sedation.** Site offers conscious sedation "for suitable patients". Left off: Mark is a fear-of-judgement persona, and the rule is no sedation language for anxious personas.
