# Mark, the lapsed patient | Meta copy

Campaign: VD | General Patients | View Content (DK Dental Practice, Weymouth). Persona: Mark (Dental Persona Bank, 01 General Dentistry key persona). 35 to 55, last check-up five or more years ago, often triggered by a twinge that hasn't gone away. Stopped by the thought of someone looking in his mouth and reacting, and by the bill he assumes is waiting. Research: research.md, sections 4 to 6.

Why Mark over Denise or Sophie: the practice's own reviews are full of his story ("dreaded going to the dentists to the point of avoiding going", "did not make me feel embarrassed", "spoke through all the payments clearly"), and the fees page answers his two blockers in writing (full costs before any work, no obligation after the exam).

Offer across all three: new patient check-up, £75. Includes a full examination, digital X-rays, oral cancer screening, a personalised written treatment plan, and home care advice. Full costs in writing before any work. No obligation to go ahead. Book through the landing page form.

Rules for this persona: no telling off, no "you should have come sooner". The cost of waiting is shown as the weight of putting it off, never as fear of disease. No sedation language. No star rating or review count on the image until the Google figure is confirmed. Reviews quoted unnamed. Statics carry hook, problem, promise and mechanism; the primary text carries the rest.

Ad names (build standard): `General Patients | Static | No talent | <Detail> | <size> | 261027`
