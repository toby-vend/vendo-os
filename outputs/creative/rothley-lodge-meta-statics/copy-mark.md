# Mark: the lapsed patient | Meta copy

Campaign: VD | New Patient | Instant Forms. Persona: Mark, 35 to 55, last visit 5+ years ago, triggered by a twinge that hasn't gone away. He's stopped by the thought of someone looking in his mouth and reacting. He expects to be judged, assumes the bill grows with the gap, and doesn't know what happens at a first appointment (Dental Persona Bank).

Offer: examination from £60, routine x-rays from £21 (fee guide, Feb 2025). Proof: 4.8 from 593 Google reviews; 29 mention a calming atmosphere and 79 caring staff (Google review topics).

Rules: no sedation language. Warm clinician over senior clinician, unpolished over polished, a quiet room over a busy one (persona bank). Never lecture. "Nobody is going to ask why you left it" is a promise the whole team has to keep, so Simon needs to sign it off.

CTA (instant form): fill in the short form and reception calls you to find a time.
