L5 · Dentures sit on bone that slowly shrinks
HEADLINE
Why dentures loosen over time.
PRIMARY TEXT
When it was first fitted, your denture felt fine. Now it needs more adhesive every year.
It isn't the denture that's changed. It's the bone underneath.
Tooth roots keep the jaw bone in use. Once they're gone, the bone slowly shrinks back, so the ridge your denture sits on gets smaller and the fit gets looser.
Implants act like new roots. The bone grows around them and keeps its shape, and your teeth or denture are fixed onto them, so they stay where they should.
