# Denise: no NHS place | Meta copy

Campaign: VD | New Patient | Instant Forms. Persona: Denise, 40 to 70. Her practice went private, or she can't get registered. She assumes private means upselling and has no idea whether a check-up is £40 or £150. Say the fee out loud, with the number on screen. "We will tell you what needs doing and what can wait." The practice manager is more credible than a clinician here (Dental Persona Bank).

Offer: examination from £60; routine x-rays from £21; hygienist (45 minutes, Airflow stain removal and Guided Biofilm Therapy) £110; Denplan payment plans with a bespoke monthly price set after the first exam (fee guide and Denplan page, Feb 2025). Proof: 4.8 from 593 Google reviews.

Rules: **never criticise the NHS.** Rothley has unhappy ex-NHS reviewers, so nothing that sounds like "we left the NHS for you". No finance figures. Denplan is a payment plan, not credit, but no monthly figure goes on the ads because the price is set per patient.

CTA (instant form): fill in the short form and reception calls you to book.
