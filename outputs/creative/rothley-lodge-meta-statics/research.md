Source: rothleylodgedentalpractice.co.uk/fee-guide ("Fees correct as of February 1st 2025"). **These fees are 20 months old. Confirm them with Andra before launch.**

- Patient examination from £60 (included on Denplan Care and Essentials).

- Private implant consultation from £199. CBCT scan from £344.
- Hygienist 45 mins, Airflow stain removal and Guided Biofilm Therapy, £110.
- Child private exam (0 to 5) is £0 when a parent is on the private or Denplan scheme. 5 to 10 years: £29. 11 to 17 years: £40.
- Dentures from £758. Crowns from £726.
- 0% finance on treatments over £500 (UK residents 18+, subject to status). **Not used on the ads:** it needs FCA wording.

- The implant open day (14 Oct) offered 10% off and a free CBCT scan. **It has ended, so neither offer is used.**

## Market position

- **Implants:** sophistication stage 4. Local and national competitors (Bupa, Portman, MyOptions per Client Bio) sell implants on from-prices and finance; Turkey sells on price. Rothley can't win on price, and doesn't publish one. Lead with mechanism (3D CBCT planning, bone loss), honest options (implant, bridge, denture or nothing) and identity (eat what you want, laugh without thinking). Proof: Simon's BACD role and the volume of reviews.
- **General:** sophistication stage 3. "Friendly family dentist" is everywhere. Rothley's unique edges are verifiable: the oldest practice in Staines, three generations of Dr Chards, four-generation patient families and 4.8 from 593 reviews. That passes the differentiator test (a proposition no competitor can claim).
- **Format:** Shaun's data says organic-looking statics with light text and a real photo outperform. The concepts below include organic formats (handwritten note, phone screenshot, plain photo with a single line) alongside branded layouts.

## Gaps (for Toby / the client)

1. What the two live instant forms actually promise (free consultation? a price?). Copy uses the fee-guide prices until confirmed.
2. Implant from-price, and whether Rothley offers same-day or immediate teeth on full arch (Geoff's strongest hook depends on it).
3. Whether a temporary tooth is fitted the same day for a lost front tooth (Nicola's hook).
4. Whether there are protected emergency slots each day (Ray). Reviews show same-day and next-day appointments happened, not that they're guaranteed.
5. Whether 2025 fees still stand.
6. Permission to quote any named reviewer.
7. Consented before-and-after cases for implants (the site has six case studies; consent for ads is unconfirmed).
