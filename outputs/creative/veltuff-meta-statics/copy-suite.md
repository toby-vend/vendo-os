# Veltuff UK Black November: creative suite copy

**Brand:** Veltuff guideline (Figma aHWliZbwJcLPtVS1PWzulV), with Veltuff's own Black November identity layered on top (`assets/src/black-november-ref.png`): outlined neon-lime "BLACK", letter-spaced "NOVEMBER", the % badge and the chevron background.
**Facts:** `offers.md`. **Research:** `research.md`.

**Rules for every ad**
- UK English. No em dashes. No emojis (Veltuff brand rule).
- No star ratings, review counts or Trustpilot.
- Never "free returns": returns are at the customer's cost.
- Prices come from Stuart's 2 Oct list. Check them on the live site before launch.
- Phase-specific dates only:
  - Prep, 1–15 Nov: teaser, no prices.
  - Category, 16–26 Nov: "up to 60% off RRP".
  - Weekend, 27–30 Nov: "30% off sitewide" with a code, excluding reduced items.
- Ad names: `<Concept> | Static | Product | <Detail> | 1x1 | YYMMDD`.
