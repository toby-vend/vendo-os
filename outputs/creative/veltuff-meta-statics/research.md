# Veltuff UK Black November: research bank

Compiled 8 Oct 2026, following the masterclass method (`data/playbooks/yt-doc/how-to-write-meta-ads-that-scale-copywriting-masterclass/`). Quotes are verbatim. **Forum posters are not Veltuff customers**, so never present them as such. Offer facts and prices are in `offers.md`.

## Proof position (read first)
- **Trustpilot: 3/5 from 400 reviews** (uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.veltuff.com). The complaints are about order picking, split deliveries and slow refunds. **Don't use Trustpilot or any star rating in ads.** It would invite people to check, and the check hurts.
- Product reviews on Okendo are few and mixed:
  - Cargo Hybrid Trousers: 13 reviews. The one visible is 2★ for fit.
  - The rest are 4–5★ but generic.
  - **No volume proof is usable.**
- Usable proof, from the site:
  - "For 25 years".
  - "Proud manufacturers for DSV, Amazon, GLS, Swissport, DFDS". Check with Stuart whether partner names can go in paid ads.
  - Certifications on specific products: EN ISO 20471 Class 2 and 3, and RIS-3279-TOM on the orange hi-vis lines.
- Material specs can stand in as the mechanism:
  - CORDURA® on wear areas (Cotton Trade Trousers).
  - Stretch knee panels and gusset (Stretch Multi-Pocket).
  - Sealed seams and a padded quilted liner (Waterproof Bomber).
  - Triple stitching at stress points (Cargo Hybrid, Teamline, Cargo Hi-Vis).
  - Gripper waistband.

## Persona 1: the tradie buying for himself (blue-collar worker in Veltuff's persona doc)
**The accommodation: he buys cheap because he knows he'll wreck them.**
> "No way I'd throw £80 at a pair of strides that'll be covered in glue, grout, Silicon and screed" (Fliselege, Tilers Forums, "best work trousers", 12 Apr 2012, https://www.tilersforums.com/threads/best-work-trousers.57988/)

> "Usually use some softshell decathlon trousers with fleece lining but at £50 they are getting ruined too quickly." (bikerevivesheffield, Singletrack, 18 Feb 2022, https://singletrackworld.com/forum/off-topic/work-trousers-whats-good/)

> "Nice trousers don't stay nice long because of oil and grease" (same thread)

> "£3 Primark jeans and a £1 foam kneeling matt from a bootsale." (Divad, Tilers Forums, 13 Apr 2012)

**The failed solution: cheap trousers split, and the pockets give up.**
> "after about 6 months, the crotch goes on them" (Bubblecraft, Tilers Forums, 13 Apr 2012)

> "the 3 pairs of trousers I have are all split in the crotch area." (Sean Kelly, Tilers Forums, 13 Apr 2012)

> "I bought some Dewalt ones and the pockets were unusable, wore them twice and put them in the charity bag" (maccruiskeen, Singletrack, 19 Feb 2022)

> "All my pockets are falling to pieces too after a good couple of years use" (z1ppy, Singletrack, 20 Feb 2022)

**The moment: knees.**
> "i used to have problems with my knees but since using these i have had no problems." (adamunited, Tilers Forums, on knee-pad trousers)

**What he values: pockets that hold, and knee pad pockets.**
> "Strong pockets are the main thing for me." (timber, Singletrack, 19 Feb 2022)

> "the knee pad pockets are good too." (Muse2k8, Tilers Forums)

**His words:** "strides", "knackered", "the crotch goes", "knee pad pockets", "split".

**Nearly didn't buy: fit. Veltuff trousers run small.**
> "I'm usually a 36 waist so ordered a 38 to be certain they would fit and I could barely get the trousers over my legs" (Clive, verified buyer, Cargo Hybrid Trousers, veltuff.co.uk, 22 Sep 2026)

Site copy for Cargo Hybrid says: "This style measures small. We advise purchasing a waist size larger than your normal measurement."
→ **Risk reversal:** tell him to size up and give the real 30-day returns window. Don't promise free returns: returns are at the customer's own cost.

**A heavier-fabric objection exists:**
> "would sell better if the product material was a little thicker which would make it harder wearing like the Apache make." (Aled Hughes, 4★, Stretch Multi-Pocket Trousers, 12 Aug 2026)

→ Don't claim "heavy duty" on the Stretch Multi-Pocket. Its edge is stretch and mobility.

**Steer away from:** Duratex™ Full Zip Work Jacket for timber trades.
> "I have never owned any garment that collected so many wood chips so fast. My new name on site is woody... caught the sleeve on a piece of floor board and it ripped very easily." (Justin Voysey, 1★, 11 May 2026)

→ Only use the Duratex fleece for the warehouse, logistics and uniform angle, never for carpentry or site work.

## Persona 2: the crew buyer (site, office or operations manager)
Source: Veltuff's own persona doc ("Office Site Manager", Drive 1_WngPjOOwNYZYcQ7TSEeNxvvqsg1W9D8I9ZKlxlcFc0) and the Fathom calls. **No public forum verbatims were found.** Treat the persona-doc lines as internal hypotheses, not customer words.
- Persona doc pain points:
  - "Affordability and remaining within workwear budget"
  - "Don't have time to reach out to multiple suppliers to get logos"
  - "They need a workwear jacket that is size inclusive... Don't want to outsource just because they can't get an XXL for Joe"
  - "Constantly dealing with complaints"
- Stuart (Fathom, 11 Aug 2026, https://fathom.video/calls/780198052): "streamlining your company-issued workwear" is the best lead-gen theme. The hook direction was "savings and hidden costs", e.g. "save 30% off your workwear".
- US forum context (Mike Holt, https://forums.mikeholt.com/threads/uniforms.68145/): one contractor supplies "5 golf shirts on day one and 5 replacements every 6 months". This is a US source, so use it for context only.
- **Product facts that fit:**
  - Two Tone Softshell Jacket, sold as "for perfect employee uniforms", in 6 colourways.
  - Duratex™ 1/4 Zip Sweatshirt and Polo, "popular choice for uniforms", tagged Add Logo.
  - Logo embroidery is available. The May brief quoted a 10–15 working day lead time with a logo.
  - Sizes from XS upwards.
- **The moment:** the start of winter, when the crew is cold and asking. Black November happens to sit at the point where Q4 budgets get spent.
- **Open:** does the BF price apply to logo orders? What does embroidery cost, and what's the lead time in November? Can a logo order land before Christmas?

- **Nearly didn't buy:** the size, and whether site rules allow the colour. Gift guides note that some sites restrict colours (https://www.regatta.com/destination-outdoors/christmas-gifts-for-workers).
  → Steer her to non-hi-vis outerwear and trousers, and give "check the label in his current pair" sizing advice.
- **Open:** gift receipts, Christmas returns window, last order date for Christmas delivery. None of these were found on the site.

## Awareness and sophistication (whole set)
- **Market:** UK budget workwear (Screwfix own-brand, Scruffs, Dickies, Apache, Amazon cargo). It's at **stage 3–4**: every brand claims "tough" and "triple stitched".
- **The edge isn't the claim.** It's the specific material plus a real price gap (CORDURA® wear areas on £70-RRP trousers, now £21).
- **Black November buyers** are mostly **product aware / most aware** for retargeting and DPA, and **solution aware** for prospecting. The prep phase (1–15 Nov) talks to the **problem aware**.
