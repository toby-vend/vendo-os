For 25 years VELTUFF® has combined safety with style, giving people workwear that is truly up to the task.

The range covers work clothing, hi-vis, safety footwear and PPE, each designed for protection, durability and comfort, whether you work in a warehouse, drive a commercial vehicle or maintain the railways.

Premium quality at an honest price. Direct, no-nonsense, built for UK trades and the teams that kit them out.

01
Logo

The VELTUFF wordmark is cut from heavy square letterforms with chamfered corners. Lime on black is the primary version.

On lime use black. On photography use white or lime over a darkened area. The V roundel is for avatars, favicons and small placements.

Clear space on every side equals the width of the V. Minimum width 120px on screen.

Supplied as PNG from the website. Ask Veltuff for vector (SVG/AI) master files and the roundel artwork.
