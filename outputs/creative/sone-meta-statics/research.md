## 5. Research per persona

### Overwhelmed Owner (single-site principal, 35 to 55)
- **Accommodation:** "video" has sat on the to-do list for a year; the nurse or TCO films the odd Reel on a phone [D2][D4].
- **Failed solution:** filmed once with no plan, it flopped [D5][S2].
- **Nearly didn't buy:** losing a clinical day [S1]; not knowing what to say on camera [Q1].
- **The word:** "content". "I know I should be doing content" [D1].
- **The moment:** a competitor's page looks better than theirs.
- **The after:** a library of videos on the site and ads, without it becoming a second job.

### Growth-Mode Principal (already running ads, may have an editor or agency)
- **Accommodation:** ads run on stock or designed graphics [D7].
- **Failed solution:** generic videographer who didn't know the surgery [D6]; footage that "looks nice but doesn't book appointments" (Ad Scripts).
- **Nearly didn't buy:** paying for footage their editor can't use. Answer: shot list planned around where each clip will run, raw footage option at £750.
- **The word:** "creative", "footage", "ads".
- **The moment:** ad performance stalls; creative fatigue.
- **The after:** a bank of real-practice footage built to run as ads.

### Cosmetic Clinician (bonding, veneers, Invisalign, implants)
- **Accommodation:** great cases, but online it's phone clips (Ad Scripts "Cosmetic Revenue Chaser").
- **Failed solution:** before-and-afters alone; stock imagery [D7].
- **Nearly didn't buy:** feeling stiff on camera [D8][Q1].
- **The word:** "cases", "results", "my work".
- **The moment:** a patient says they chose someone else after watching their videos (Frame 11 logic: patients shortlist after watching videos).
- **The after:** treatment explainers and patient stories that match the quality in the chair.

### Practice Going Private (NHS to private conversion, or associate opening a private list)
- **Accommodation:** relying on the existing patient base; website still reads like the old practice.
- **Failed solution:** a letter or leaflet to patients explaining the change.
- **Nearly didn't buy:** spending on marketing during a risky transition.
- **The word:** "going private", "fees", "the list".
- **The moment:** the conversion date is set.
- **The after:** new patients who already know the dentist's face and what private care looks like there before they book.
- Source: existing Frame 06 "GOING PRIVATE? High-value patients watch before they enquire"; Ad Scripts persona hooks ("Associates about to go private, the content you make in year one decides how fast you fill the diary").

## 6. Market sophistication

Dental video is a crowded offer (generic videographers, agencies, UGC). Plain claims ("professional dental video") are stage 1 and spent. Sone's edge sits at stage 3 to 4: the mechanism is plan first (brief, script, direction, in-house edit), from a dental-only crew. Identity lines (stage 5) close each ad.
