Campaign: VD | Instant Forms | Leads (Sone Marketing). Persona: single-site principal, 35 to 55, seeing a full list every day. Knows they "should be doing content", has had video on the to-do list for a year, and the odd Reel gets filmed on a nurse's phone. Biggest fears: losing a clinical day, and not knowing what to say on camera. Research: research.md, section 5.

Offer across all five: one shoot day at the practice, from £750 + VAT, or £1,250 + VAT with editing and scripting support. Edited videos back in 2 to 3 weeks. Book a discovery call.

Rules for this persona: no guilt about being behind. The cost of waiting is practical (competitors' pages, the list staying on the to-do list), never a telling off. Statics carry hook, problem, promise and mechanism. Primary text carries the rest.
