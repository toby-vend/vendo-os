# Practice Going Private | Meta copy

Campaign: VD | Instant Forms | Leads (Sone Marketing). Persona: an owner converting from NHS to private, or an associate opening a private list. The date is set. Their worry: will patients stay, and will new ones find them? Their website still reads like the old practice. The failed solution is a letter or leaflet to patients. Research: research.md, section 5.

Offer across all five: shoot day from £750 + VAT, or £1,250 + VAT edited with scripting support. Book a discovery call.

Rules: never knock the NHS or NHS dentists. Never promise patient numbers or retention. The angle is trust and familiarity: new private patients want to know who they're paying, and video is how they meet you first.
