Campaign: VD | Instant Forms | Leads (Sone Marketing). Persona: dentist doing composite bonding, veneers, Invisalign or implants. Clinically proud of their cases. Online, those cases show up as phone clips or before-and-after stills. Worried they'll look stiff on camera. Patients for these treatments shortlist practices by watching video before they enquire (Sone's Frame 11 and 15 copy). Research: research.md, section 5.

Offer across all five: edited package from £1,250 + VAT with scripting support (lead offer for this persona: they need finished explainers, not raw footage), or shoot day from £750 + VAT. Book a discovery call.

Rules: no before-and-afters on the ads (Sone has no consented cases for its own ads). No patient-outcome claims. Treatment names only as content topics, never as promises.
