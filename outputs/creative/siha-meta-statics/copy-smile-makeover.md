# Smile makeover: align, whiten, bond | Meta copy

Campaign: Teeth Straightening (aligners, ortho and bonding options). Persona: Michelle, the long-term self-conscious patient (Dental Persona Bank, Smile Makeover key persona). She's 40 to 60, has wanted this for 15 to 20 years, and is triggered by a milestone or the end of her patience. Her barriers are permission, not clinical: she thinks she's too old, that it's vain, and that it'll cost a fortune.

The makeover, in Hannan's words on the 2 Oct onboarding call: "whitening, six to nine months of aligners, and two to four teeth of composite bonding rather than ten veneers". Order used in the ads: align, then whiten, then bond, because bonding doesn't whiten afterwards (Siha recommends whitening before resin work).

Offer: free smile makeover consultation (complimentary on Siha's fees page). Bonding from £250 a tooth. No aligner or whitening prices (aligners rose £200 on 1 October; whitening prices are marked limited-time). Research: research.md (smile makeover section).
