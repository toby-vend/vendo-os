# Niamh: high functioning and anxious | Meta copy

Campaign: General, ICON / whitening. Persona: Niamh, 25 to 50. Competent and in control everywhere else; can't do this one thing. She isn't avoiding treatment, she's avoiding unpredictability. She'll pay privately and stay for years with a practice that hands her control before she has to ask (Dental Persona Bank).

Rules from the bank, kept on every asset:
- Mechanism first, reassurance second, offer third.
- Never say "relax". Never use sedation language: "you won't remember a thing" repels her, because losing control is the fear itself. Siha doesn't advertise sedation on its site, so none is claimed.
- The stop signal is the highest-converting sentence for her. State it plainly.
- Online booking, not only the phone.

Offer: new patient check-up, £89, with everything explained before it happens. Research: research.md (Niamh section).
