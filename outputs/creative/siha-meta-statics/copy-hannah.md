She wants straight, clean, a shade brighter and still clearly her own teeth. Her fear is the result, not the procedure, and she can't name the treatments (Dental Persona Bank). Offer across all five: free cosmetic consultation; bonding from £250 a tooth where it's needed. Research: research.md (Hannah section).

Statics carry hook, problem, promise and mechanism. Primary text carries the rest of the nine beats. Image, headline and primary text start from the same awareness stage.

## H3 | Move them, don't file them
**Awareness:** solution aware. **Sophistication:** 3 to 4 (mechanism). **Angle:** the order of treatment is the difference.
Research: people describe the smiles they admire as "a nice smile that's benefited from good orthodontics & whitening"; "They don't realise what they have lost by hacking away at their own teeth" (Mumsnet [5]); "if you ever get your teeth whitened, the composite bonding won't whiten" (Mumsnet [3]).

**On image:** We'd rather move your teeth than file them. / 1 Straighten / 2 Whiten / 3 Bond, only where it's needed / Book a free consultation

**Headline:** Straighten. Whiten. Then bond.

**Primary text:**
Most of the smiles people admire weren't filed down. They were straightened and whitened.

That's the order we work in.

First, straighten if your teeth need it. Our aligner cases are designed by an orthodontist, and a 3D scan shows you the predicted result before you commit.

Then whiten, so any bonding is matched to your new shade, not your old one. Bonding doesn't whiten afterwards, so the order matters.

Then bond, only where an edge or a gap still needs it. Resin is added to the tooth, so nothing healthy is filed away. From £250 a tooth.

It takes longer than a week abroad. It also leaves you with your own teeth.

Book a free consultation and we'll tell you which steps you actually need. Often it's fewer than you think.
