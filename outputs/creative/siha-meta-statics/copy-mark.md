# Mark: the lapsed patient | Meta copy

Campaign: General, ICON / whitening. Persona: Mark, 35 to 55, last visit five or more years ago, prompted by a twinge that hasn't gone away. He expects to be judged for the gap, assumes the bill grows with every year, doesn't know what a first appointment involves, and is waiting to feel ready (Dental Persona Bank). This persona feeds Hannan's main goal: routine patients who stay for years.

Offer across all five: new patient check-up, £89 (Hannan, onboarding call 2 Oct 2026). Research: research.md (Mark section).

Statics carry hook, problem, promise and mechanism. Primary text carries the rest. Image, headline and primary text start from the same awareness stage. Persona rule from the bank: shame closes wallets, so no line implies he has let himself go. Cost of delay is practical, never a telling off.
