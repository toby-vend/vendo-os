## Client steer from calls (shapes the copy)

- General leads are low value and time-heavy; "Many leads are price-shopping, using the practice for a checkup before seeking cheaper NHS care" (2 Jul). Many Google leads "are for NHS services, not the private treatments the ads are designed to attract" (19 Aug). Reception finds forms time-consuming, many "individuals seeking National Health Service care rather than private treatments" (8 Oct). **So the ads say "private" on the image and put the £65 fee up front.**
- "The 'all x-rays included' ad copy caused patient friction by implying OPGs were free" (2 Jul). **So the ads say "2 small X-rays", the fees page wording, never "X-rays included".**
- Free consultations were switched off after negative comments about the £65 deposit; "all static ads and landing pages have been updated to remove references to free consultations" (8 Oct). **So nothing here says free.**
- A mismatched visual (a scaling image on an aligner ad) went live without approval and upset the client (19 Aug). These statics need ARC's sign-off before launch.

## Customer language (third-party forums, never presented as ARC patients)

Verbatim Mumsnet quotes, checked word for word when compiled 19 Oct 2026 (full table: outputs/creative/concept-dental-meta-statics/research-forums.md, Mark section).

- [M1] Nearly didn't / own words: "I keep wanting to go to a new dentist but I'm so anxious about what they're going to say and I just feel like they're going to humiliate me and tell me off" ([link](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/am_i_being_unreasonable/3408764-Embarrassed-to-go-to-the-dentist-about-my-bad-teeth))
- [M3] Accommodation: "Through the 20 years I didn't go I became more and more embarrassed of my crooked teeth and as a result I never smile or laugh with them showing" ([link](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/_chat/3795679-Today-I-went-to-the-dentist-for-the-1st-time-in-18-years))
- [M4] Moment: "Then one day I finally just thought- FUCK IT and I plucked up the courage to make the decision to change my ways and do something about it." (same thread)
- [M5] The word: "Most of the reason I can't bring myself to go is shame/embarrassment rather than fear at this point tbh" ([link](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/_chat/3795679-Today-I-went-to-the-dentist-for-the-1st-time-in-18-years?page=2))
- [M6] Accommodation and after: "I've put it off for years and buried my head in the sand and been terrified that my teeth are awful. Eventually went today and I need fillings but nowhere near as bad or expensive as I thought." ([link](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/_chat/4919103-how-long-since-youve-been-to-the-dentist))
- [M9] After: "I didn't see a dentist for about 10 years and when I finally plucked up the courage to go I was embarrassed to admit how long it had been … he didn't judge me" ([link](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/am_i_being_unreasonable/4102415-To-ask-if-the-dentist-will-judge-me))
- Persona bank (Mark): "Expects to be judged for the gap in his history"; "Assumes the longer he has left it, the bigger the bill"; "Does not know what happens at a first appointment"; "Waiting to feel 'ready' and never is".

Patterns: shame outranks fear; the decision is framed as courage ("plucked up the courage"); time makes it worse ("put it off for years", "buried my head in the sand"); the after is relief that it was "nowhere near as bad or expensive" as feared.
