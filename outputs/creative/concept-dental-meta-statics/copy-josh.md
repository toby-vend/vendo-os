Will composite bonding stain? Yes, it can.

You've probably read that on a forum already, along with "it chips" and "it won't last". Some of that's fair, so here's the straight version.

Tea, coffee and tobacco can stain bonding over time. A cosmetic polish helps keep it looking fresh, and regular hygiene visits help too. If a bit chips, it can be repaired, rather than started again. And you can help it along by not using your teeth to open things.

Why do people choose it anyway? Because it's added to your own teeth with little or no preparation, it's often done in a single visit, and you keep the teeth underneath. Veneers involve preparing the tooth, which can't be reversed.
