**Headline:** Bonding or veneers? Know the difference.

**Primary text:**
Before anyone touches your teeth, you should know this.

Composite bonding is added to your tooth. It usually needs little or no preparation, it's often done in a single visit, and if it ever chips it can be repaired.

Veneers involve preparing the front of the tooth first. That can't be reversed. They're the right answer for some smiles, but it's a decision you make once.

Most people looking online don't know the difference. They pick a price or a photo, and find out what was involved afterwards.
