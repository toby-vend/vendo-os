# Barry: terrified of the surgery | Meta copy

**Campaign:** Implants (£500/mo).
**Persona:** Barry, 30 to 65. He's been told an implant is the right answer and agrees with his head, but he won't book. He has read everything that can go wrong, and the specific fear is the drilling (Dental Persona Bank: micro-persona Barry).
**Offer:** implant consultation, with a written plan and confirmed costs before committing. Single implant £2,500 including CBCT scan and crown (https://conceptdental.co.uk/fees/).
**Research:** `research-forums.md` (Barry).

**Rules for this persona:**
- **No sedation language** (Toby's standing rule for anxious personas). The persona bank recommends sedation as Barry's main route; we've built around control, information and the steps instead. Flagged in Notes.
- Never show surgical footage or imagery.
- No "pain-free" claims.
- A patient describing the day is the strongest asset, once a consented one exists. Until then, process and control.
