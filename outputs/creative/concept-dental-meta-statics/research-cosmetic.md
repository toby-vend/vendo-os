## Market

- Local area (Broxtowe, Census 2021): median age 43, 71% homeowners. Fewer 25 to 40s than the national mix, so cosmetic targeting should lean on interest signals rather than a tight age band.
- Sophistication stage 3 to 4. Cosmetic buyers have seen "transform your smile" claims and Turkey-teeth horror stories. Lead with mechanism (add, don't drill; confirmed price in writing; a dentist examines you) and identity (still your teeth).
