## Brand (from the guidelines PDF and the site)

- Colours: deep blue `#2B3A4C`, magenta `#A81F64`, turquoise `#65C5B7`, caramel `#BB9763` (gold foil in print), stone `#EDE5DA`. Preferred pairings: stone on any brand colour, or any colour on stone.
- Type: ES Serif Regular for headings, in sentence case. ES Script is for one or two decorative words only, never key information. Montserrat for body copy.
- ES Serif came from the site's embedded webfont (98 glyphs, no £ sign, so prices fall back to a serif).

## Persona: Mark, the lapsed patient (Dental Persona Bank, 01 General Dentistry)

35 to 55. Last visit five or more years ago, triggered by a twinge that won't go away. Expects to be judged for the gap, assumes the bill grows every year, doesn't know what a first appointment involves, and is waiting to feel "ready". Casting rules from the bank: a warm clinician over a senior one, and an empty waiting room rather than a busy one.

Why Mark for General: he's the key General Dentistry persona, and the rebrand gives a true fresh-start angle (new name, same practice). Sally's own patient base skews 35 to 55 (onboarding call).
