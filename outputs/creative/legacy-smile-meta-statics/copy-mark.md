# Mark: the lapsed patient | Legacy Smile | General | Meta copy

**Persona:** Mark, 35 to 55. Last check-up five or more years ago, and a twinge that hasn't gone away. He expects a telling off for the gap, assumes the bill grows every year, doesn't know what a first appointment involves, and is waiting to feel ready (Dental Persona Bank).

**Campaign:** General (new patients).

**Offer on all three:** New Patient Examination, £110 (fee guide, updated 11 Dec 2025).

**Research:** research.md.

**Rules for this persona:**
- No line implies he's let himself go.
- The cost of waiting is practical, never a lecture.
- No sedation language.
- No star rating, because the average is unverified. Proof is "Over 300 Google reviews".

Statics carry the hook, problem, promise and mechanism. The primary text carries the rest.
