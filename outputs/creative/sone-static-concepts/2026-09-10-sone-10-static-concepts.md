# Sone Productions — 10 New Static Concepts

**Date:** 10 September 2026
**Source set:** Drive → Sone Productions → Content Assets → Static Creatives (22 files, 11 concepts × 4:5 + 9:16)
**Audience:** Principals and owners of UK private dental practices
**Constraint applied:** every concept below is built only on facts already proven in the existing set. Nothing invented. Where a concept needs an asset Sone already owns (footage, diary), it is flagged.

---

## Part 1 — What the current set does, and where the gaps are

### The 11 existing concepts

| # | Angle | Message |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | Social proof | 750+ dental content shoot days completed |
| 2 | Price | £750. Full day at your practice |
| 3 | Ease | We show up. We film. We deliver. |
| 4 | Pain | Cosmetic patients choose based on video |
| 5 | Process | We don't start with a camera. We start with a plan (1–4) |
| 6 | Pain + price | GOING PRIVATE? High-value patients watch before they enquire |
| 7 | Pain | You filmed once. Then what? |
| 8 | Offer | Video content built to run as ads. One day, every channel |
| 9 | Positioning | Don't film more. Film Smarter |
| 10 | Brand | Your practice. On camera. Done right. |
| 11 | Deliverables | Video that does the work (explainers / testimonials / team) |

Frames 03, 04, 09 and 10 are missing from the folder.

### The nine gaps

1. **A videography company running zero visuals.** All 22 are flat typographic colour blocks. Sone is asking a principal to spend £1,250 on production quality while showing not one frame of its own work. This is the single largest gap in the set and the easiest to close.
2. **One proof point in the whole set.** "750+ shoot days" carries all the credibility on its own. It is stated, never shown.
3. **No quantified deliverable.** "One day, every channel" gives a prospect nothing to judge value against.
4. **Objections untouched.** Will a crew wreck my clinical day? Do I have to be on camera? Will my team freeze? Who writes it? Only concept 3 gestures at this, abstractly.
5. **Audience call-out nearly absent.** Only "GOING PRIVATE?" names anyone. Implants, Invisalign, full-mouth rehab and cosmetic sit buried in body copy, never used as the hook.
6. **Price is naked.** £750 appears with no anchor and no reframe. A number on its own invites comparison shopping.
7. **No geography and no urgency**, despite a crew that physically travels to the practice. Regional routing is an obvious lever going unused.
8. **Zero format variety.** Logo top, headline middle, CTA bottom, eleven times over. In feed these will fatigue as one creative, not eleven.
9. **Six of eleven carry no CTA at all**, and every CTA that exists is the same high-friction "Book A Discovery Call". There is no step for the 90% who are interested but not ready to talk.

## Part 2 — The 10 new concepts

Each concept states the gap it closes. Copy is final unless marked. Set 4:5 and 9:16 for every one.

---

### 01 — The Specialist Wedge
**Closes:** gap 5 (no differentiation, no audience call-out)
**Funnel:** cold, pattern interrupt

> **Your last videographer had never seen a surgery before.**
>
> They didn't know what a scan looks like. They didn't know where they could and couldn't stand. They didn't think about the patient in the background.
>
> We only film dentistry. 750+ shoot days, every one of them in a practice.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** Black ground. Headline dominant over four lines, yellow full stop. Yellow rule separating the accusation from the resolution. Body in white at roughly a third of headline size.
**Why it works:** this is the only concept in either set that answers "why you rather than any videographer with a camera". It converts the specialism from a strapline into an argument.
**Needs:** nothing.

---

### 02 — The Clinical Day Objection
**Closes:** gap 4 (the top unspoken reason principals say no)
**Funnel:** warm, objection handling

> **The reason you keep putting it off isn't the money.**
>
> It's the picture in your head. A crew in the corridor. Cables across reception. A nurse standing about while someone resets a light. A full list, running late, and a waiting room that notices.
>
> So it moves to next quarter. Again.
>
> We plan the day around your list. Not the other way round.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** Cream ground, editorial. Headline set tight and large. Body as three short stacked paragraphs with generous leading. Final line isolated at the bottom in black bold.
**Why it works:** £750 is not a hard budget decision for a private practice. The real blocker is operational disruption, and nothing in the current set acknowledges it exists.
**Needs:** nothing.

---

### 03 — The Camera-Shy Principal
**Closes:** gap 4 (objection), gap 1 (no human element anywhere in the set)
**Funnel:** warm

> **You hate the sound of your own voice.**
>
> That is not a reason to have no video.
>
> Most principals we film have never been in front of a camera. We script it, we direct it, and we shoot it until it sounds like you.
>
> And if you would rather not be in it at all, we build the day around your team instead.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** Yellow ground. First line black and dominant. Second line smaller, italic, sitting directly beneath as the turn. Body block below a black rule.
**Why it works:** it names a private, slightly embarrassing objection out loud, which is the fastest way to earn attention from the exact person who has been quietly avoiding this for two years.
**Needs:** nothing.

---

### 04 — The Price Reframe
**Closes:** gap 6 (naked price), gap 3 (vague deliverable)
**Funnel:** consideration

> **£750 is not a video.**
> **It's a day.**
>
> A full day in your practice with a crew that films nothing but dentistry. Treatment explainers. Patient testimonials. Team videos.
>
> Shoot only — £750 + VAT
> Shoot + edits — £1,250 + VAT

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** Black ground. "£750" in yellow at maximum scale, "is not a video" in white beneath at a third the size, "It's a day." in yellow. Two-column price block at the base following the frame 15/16 pattern.
**Why it works:** frames 05, 06, 15 and 16 all put the number up naked, which invites the prospect to compare it against a freelancer's day rate. This reframes what the number buys before that comparison can happen.
**Needs:** nothing.

### 05 — The Shoot Day Timetable
**Closes:** gap 3 (unquantified deliverable), gap 8 (format variety)
**Funnel:** consideration

> **One day. Here's what it actually looks like.**
>
> 09:00 — We arrive and set up
> 10:00 — Treatment explainers
> 12:30 — Patient testimonials
> 14:30 — Team videos
> 16:30 — We wrap and you get your practice back
>
> Shoot only £750 + VAT. Shoot + edits £1,250 + VAT.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** Cream ground. Reads as a schedule, not an ad. Times in yellow-filled black circles echoing the numbered markers on frames 13/14. Left-aligned, monospaced feel on the times.
**Why it works:** "One day, every channel" is the vaguest claim in the current set. A running order makes the day concrete and simultaneously answers the disruption objection without arguing about it.
**Needs:** Sone to confirm the real running order. This is their own process, not a claim to invent.

### 06 — Full-Bleed Film Still
**Closes:** gap 1 (the biggest gap in the set), gap 8 (format variety)
**Funnel:** cold

> **This was a Tuesday.**
>
> Dental videography. One day at your practice. From £750 + VAT.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** A single strong still from a real shoot, full bleed. Dark gradient across the bottom third. Logo top left in white. One headline and one line of subcopy only. No yellow block, no boxed CTA competing with the image.
**Why it works:** every other ad in the account asks a prospect to trust Sone's production quality on assertion alone. This one demonstrates it in the first half second, and the understated line lets the frame carry the argument.
**Needs:** one strong still from the existing library, cleared for use.

### 07 — The Proof Grid
**Closes:** gap 1 and gap 2 (the one stat is told, never shown)
**Funnel:** cold, credibility

> **750+ shoot days.**
>
> Every one of them in a dental practice.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** 3×3 grid of stills from nine different practices, edge to edge, no gutters. Stat overlaid on a yellow block occupying the centre cell, or set beneath the grid on black. Practice names not required and probably better left off.
**Why it works:** it takes the single credibility asset already in the set and turns it from a claim into evidence, while doing double duty as a portfolio.
**Needs:** nine stills from nine distinct practices, cleared for use.

### 08 — Treatment-Segment Cuts
**Closes:** gap 5 (audience call-out buried in body copy)
**Funnel:** cold, self-selection
**One concept, three variants.**

> **a)** Implant patients don't book blind.
> **b)** Nobody commits to Invisalign off a photo.
> **c)** Full-mouth rehab patients research for months. What do they find?

Shared subcopy:
> High-value patients watch before they enquire. If what they find doesn't reflect your clinical standard, they go elsewhere.
>
> One day at your practice. From £750 + VAT.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** Black ground. Treatment word set in yellow, rest of the headline in white. Otherwise identical across the three so they read as a family in feed.
**Why it works:** frame 15 has exactly the right insight and hides it in eight lines of body copy where nobody will read it. Moving the treatment into the hook lets the right principal self-select in the first word, and gives Meta three distinct creatives off one idea.
**Needs:** nothing.

---

### 09 — Regional Routing
**Closes:** gap 7 (no geography, no urgency)
**Funnel:** cold, local relevance

> **We're filming in [REGION] this month.**
>
> Sone travels to your practice. If we're already in your area, the day is easier to book.
>
> Tell us where you are.

**CTA:** Book A Discovery Call
**Layout:** Yellow ground. Region name at maximum scale in black, swapped monthly. Small black rule and body beneath. Deliberately plain so the swap is a one-field change.
**Why it works:** a national service reads as remote. Naming the region makes it local, and the crew's actual travel schedule supplies genuine urgency without any manufactured scarcity. Rotate the region monthly and it also refreshes the creative for free.
**Needs:** Sone's shoot diary. Nothing else.

---

### 10 — The Low-Friction CTA
**Closes:** gap 9 (six of eleven have no CTA, and every CTA is the same call)
**Funnel:** cold to warm, retargeting

> **Don't book a call. Just watch one shoot day.**
>
> Sixty seconds of what a day at your practice actually produces.

**CTA:** Watch The Showreel
**Layout:** Black ground. Still frame behind at low opacity with a yellow play triangle centred. Headline beneath. CTA button in yellow, matching the existing button treatment on frames 06 and 08.
**Why it works:** "Book A Discovery Call" is a high-commitment ask aimed at the small fraction ready to talk. This catches everyone else and puts them into a retargeting pool where concepts 01 to 05 can do the persuading.
**Needs:** a 60-second showreel and a page to send traffic to.

## Part 3 — Production and testing notes

**Formats:** 4:5 and 9:16 for all ten, matching the existing naming convention.

**Colour rotation:** the existing set runs black, yellow and cream. Keep that rotation across the ten so no two consecutive concepts in a launch share a ground colour, otherwise they merge in feed.

**Run first (highest expected lift):**
1. **06 — Full-Bleed Film Still.** Closes the largest gap in the account and requires one asset Sone already owns.
2. **01 — The Specialist Wedge.** The only differentiation argument in either set.
3. **08 — Treatment-Segment Cuts.** Three creatives off one build, and the sharpest audience self-selection.

**Hold for later:** 09 and 10 both depend on assets or scheduling outside the creative itself.

**What this set deliberately does not do:** no client names, no testimonial quotes, no result figures and no asset counts, because none of those are established anywhere in the current creative. If Sone can supply a cleared client name, a testimonial or a result, three or four further concepts open up immediately, and they would likely outperform everything above.
