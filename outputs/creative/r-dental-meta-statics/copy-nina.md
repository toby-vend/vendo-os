# Nina, fixing work done abroad | R-Dental Meta copy

**Campaign:** Cosmetic (the Meta smile makeover / Smile Restoration campaign, £500/month, ~4 miles around Edgware). No Media Plan tab found; campaign taken from the 6 Aug, 10 Sep and 30 Sep calls (research.md).

**Persona:** Nina (Dental Persona Bank, Smile Makeover micro-persona). 25 to 55, had veneers or implants abroad. Something has chipped, come loose, never settled or just doesn't look like her. Embarrassed, expecting a lecture, so she puts it off. What moves her: no judgement, said plainly and early, and an honest answer about what can and can't be fixed.

**Why this persona for R-Dental:** it's the angle Dev asked for ("the center where you've gone to an aesthetic dentist and it's failed"), and the proof is real and hard to copy: a hospital Consultant in Restorative Dentistry does the assessment.

**Offer used:** a consultation (no price on the image). Primary text names the £225 prosthodontic consultation with x-rays from the fee guide. Proof: Rated Excellent, 34 Google reviews.

**Rules for this persona:**
- Never name a country, a clinic or "Turkey teeth". Never knock other dentists (Dev: "not throwing shade"). The fault is never hers.
- Never call R-Dental "aesthetic" or "cosmetic dentists" (Mrs Ranka's preference).
- No before-and-afters, no damaged-teeth close-ups, no alarm. No stock. Imagery is the practice's own: the team photo and Mrs Ranka's portrait from r-dental.uk.
- No bonding or veneer prices (both conflict, see Notes). No finance. No urgency.
- Forum and press quotes shape the language only. They are never presented as R-Dental patients.

**Talent:** the client's own photography from r-dental.uk: the team photo (five of the team in the practice, Mrs Ranka centre) on N1, and Mrs Ranka's portrait (bgOwner.webp) on N3, where the copy names her. N2 is type-led. No stock. There is no patient or lifestyle photography of a Nina-age patient, so the problem is carried by the copy, not shown in the imagery (shot list in research.md).
