## Persona choice

**Nina, fixing work done abroad** (Dental Persona Bank, 02 Smile Makeover micro-persona; Drive Dental_Persona_Bank_Master.docx). "Twenty five to fifty five, had veneers or implants abroad, something has debonded, failed or looks wrong. Embarrassed and expecting judgement... No judgement, only solutions, said plainly and early. 'Had work done abroad and something is not right?' What can be fixed and what cannot, honestly. Bring any records from the original treatment... Private feeling and undramatic, never alarmist case study imagery."

Why Nina over Michelle (the key smile makeover persona): it is the one smile-makeover angle the client asked for in their own words (complications from aesthetic work, locals going abroad), and it passes the differentiator test. 50 practices within three miles can sell veneers; few can put a hospital consultant in restorative dentistry on the case. Michelle is the fallback if Toby wants a broader aesthetic set.
