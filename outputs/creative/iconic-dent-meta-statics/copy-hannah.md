## Notes: checks for the client
1. **Timings (H1, H3, H5):** "whitening and bonding in a few weeks, aligners a few months or more" comes from the 4 Oct shoot plan script and the site FAQ. Confirm the team is happy with this wording.
2. **"Whiten first, then bond to match" (H2):** a common sequence, and from a forum thread [MN6]. Confirm this is how Iconic plans it. If not, we'll cut the line.
3. **"No drilling down" (H4):** composite bonding is usually additive. Confirm the team is happy with this claim, or we'll drop it.
4. **Bonding price, packages, free consultation, seeing the smile first:** same checks as Michelle (notes 1 to 4).
5. **Aligners:** the landing page offers Invisalign. No aligner price is on any Hannah ad.
6. **Wedding imagery:** no wedding stock. The envelope, notepad and wax seal are drawn in the design, not photos. If the 4 Oct shoot has any engaged or bride-to-be patients (with consent), they'd be stronger.
