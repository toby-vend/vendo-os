# Mark: back after years away | Meta copy

Campaign: VD | General Patient | View Content (General Patient and Nervous Patient ad sets). Landing page: lp.iconicdent.co.uk/new-patients-offer-meta/

Persona: Mark, 35 to 55. Last check-up five, ten, twenty years ago. Something has started to twinge and won't settle. What stops him isn't the chair. It's the thought of someone looking in his mouth and reacting, a lecture, and a bill that grew while he wasn't looking (Dental Persona Bank).

Offer across all five: new patient check-up £40 (x-rays £15 each only if needed), a written plan with costs, and nothing beyond the exam booked or charged until he agrees the plan [S1, S6]. Open Saturdays. 5.0 from 125 Google reviews [G0]. Research: research.md (Mark).

Rules for Mark:
- No sedation language (skill rule for anxious personas).
- No named reviews or verbatim review quotes until the reviewers give permission. Use the volume proof and paraphrase themes only.
- Unpolished, warm production. Real practice photos only: no CGI renders, no stock.
- Norwich only, no suburbs.
