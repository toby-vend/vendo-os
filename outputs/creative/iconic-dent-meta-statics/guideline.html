Who we are
Private Dentist Norwich

Iconic Dent is a private practice at 2 Magdalen Street in central Norwich, run by two directors, Dr Lana Nashef and Dr Lana Haj Yahya. It covers check-ups and hygiene, emergency appointments, implants and dentures, Invisalign and clear aligners, bonding, veneers, whitening and smile makeovers.

The practice exists “to help every patient feel understood, cared for, and fully confident in their smile.” Three membership plans spread routine care across the year.

Logo
The Logo

A ring holding an angular monogram of fine strokes, over ICONIC DENT in widely set capitals. The stacked lockup is the primary. The horizontal lockup suits narrow headers; the monogram alone is the sign on the reception wall, a watermark and an avatar.

Clear space on every side equals the height of the wordmark capitals. Minimum width 110px on screen, 25mm in print.

Charcoal on Paper, Linen or White. Linen or White on Charcoal and dark photography. Gold where the practice uses it: the brushed gold sign. Never retype the wordmark or redraw the ring.

Iconic is light, warm and quiet: Linen and Paper carry most of the space, Charcoal is for dark sections and evening-toned ads, and Gold is the single accent. Roughly 60% Linen and Paper, 20% Charcoal, 10% White, 10% Gold. Gold is used solid (buttons, discs, ticks) or as the photographed brushed-gold sign, never as a gradient or a glow. Headlines are Black on light grounds and Linen on Charcoal. No clinical blues, no bright colours.

07
Photography
Our Practice, Our People

Real people in the real practice on Magdalen Street: the team in stone-coloured scrubs, the gold sign on the marble wall, the reception and lounge, the surgeries and the CBCT room.

The site's interior renders are CGI and its smiling-model photos are stock. Neither goes into ads. Name a team member only when the practice confirms it.

Look for
Warm uplight, marble, brushed gold and soft stone tones. The team at work, calm conversations at the screen, the branded details. Patients who look relaxed, not posed.
Avoid
Stock models, CGI renders, harsh clinical white, needles and gloved hands in mouths, and before-and-afters without the patient's written consent.

08
Tone of voice
How We Sound
Calm, warm and plain-spoken: we listen, we explain, we care. Never salesy, never clinical. UK English, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no outcome guarantees or “pain-free” claims, no finance figures without FCA wording, and urgency only when an offer is genuinely live.
Transparent
We say
You’ll get your options and costs in writing.
We don’t say
Unbeatable prices! Book NOW!
Reassuring
We say
We go at your pace and explain every step.
We don’t say
Pain-free dentistry, guaranteed.
Local
We say
Your dentist on Magdalen Street, in the heart of Norwich.
We don’t say
The best dentist in East Anglia.
Personal
We say
Tell us what you’d like to change, and we’ll show you what’s possible.
We don’t say
Get the perfect Hollywood smile.
