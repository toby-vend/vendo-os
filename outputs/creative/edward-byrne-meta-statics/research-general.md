## What the research says for Mark
- **Word:** "left it", "years", "scolded", "judge".
- **Accommodation:** waiting to "feel ready" (persona bank), the gap getting longer and harder to break ("The longer I didn't go the harder it was").
- **Failed solution:** waiting for the twinge to go; meaning to book.
- **Nearly didn't buy:** being told off. Answer: the practice's own words, "we'll take things at your pace, explaining everything as we go", and 45-minute appointments.
- **Moment:** a twinge that doesn't go away.
