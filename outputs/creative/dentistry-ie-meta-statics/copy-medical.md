## Artane

### We Accept Medical Cards

**Headlines:**
- Medical card patients welcome
- Medical card dentist in Artane
- Your dental care is covered

**Primary text:**

Have a medical card? We’ll see you.

Lots of people with a medical card don’t realise it covers dental treatment, or they’ve been told “we don’t take medical cards” and given up asking.

At Dentistry.ie Artane, we welcome medical card patients. Your card covers essential treatment, including a dental examination, fillings and extractions.

100% Irish owned, and caring for families since 1980.

Our Artane clinic is at Artane Castle Shopping Centre. Tap Book Now, fill in the short form, and the team will call you to arrange your appointment.

Your entitlements, used properly.
