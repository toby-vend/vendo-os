### One Plan

**Headlines:**
- One plan for your whole smile
- Smile makeovers in Artane
- Straighten, whiten, then refine

**Primary text:**

Fixing your smile one tooth at a time rarely looks right.

Whitening on its own won’t fix a crooked tooth. Bonding on crowded teeth won’t sit evenly. The best results usually come from doing things in the right order.

That’s why we plan your whole smile together. A typical plan might be Invisalign to straighten first, then whitening, then bonding to refine the edges. Every step builds on the last, and the finish looks natural.

Your consultation in Artane includes a digital scan and photos, and you get a clear, written plan with every cost set out before anything starts. 0% finance is available, subject to status and terms and conditions.

100% Irish owned, and caring for families since 1980.
