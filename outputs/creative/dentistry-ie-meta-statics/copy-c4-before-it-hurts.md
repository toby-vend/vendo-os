# C4 | Before it hurts | Dentistry.ie general statics | Meta copy

**Persona:** Mark / Ray (Dental Persona Bank): only goes when something hurts. **Awareness:** problem aware. **Sophistication:** 4 (catch it early: the exam looks for early decay and wear). **Angle:** come in before the pain decides for you.

**Research:** "I only go when I have bad cavities and toothaches" (Boards.ie, 2017); "I have a constant dull pain that I think I've just got my brain to be able to tune out" (Boards.ie, 2020). Client: the exam checks "to assess your bite, identify any signs of decay, and spot early wear and tear" (clinic landing pages).

**On image:** New patients welcome in [area] / Only go when something hurts? *Come in before it does.* / A full check-up looks for early signs of decay and wear, while they're still small. / Book your check-up. Sky ground, smiling clinician.

## Artane

**Headlines:**
- Don’t wait for the toothache
- New patients welcome in Artane

**Primary text:**

If you only go to the dentist when something hurts, you’re not alone.

Maybe there’s a twinge you’ve learned to tune out. Maybe you’re waiting to see if it settles. The trouble is, a tooth often only starts to hurt once a problem has been there a while.

A check-up is the easy way to stay ahead of it. Your first visit at our Artane clinic is a full exam that looks for early signs of decay, gum problems and wear, plus a mouth cancer screening. We explain what we find in plain English, and nothing goes ahead until you’re ready.

Over 500 five-star Google reviews across our eight clinics, and caring for families since 1980.

Our Artane clinic is at Artane Castle Shopping Centre, and new patients are welcome. Tap Book Now, leave your details, and the team will be in touch to find a time.

Come in before it hurts, not because it does.
