## Client facts used (landing pages, read 23 Oct 2026)

Implants LP (all clinics, same template):
- "implants eliminate the slipping, clicking, and gum irritation often associated with removable dentures"
- single implant "preserves the health of your surrounding teeth"; options from single tooth to "Full Arch (All on 4)"
- journey: consultation with the treatment coordinator; "X-rays and a CBCT scan"; placement as "a minor surgical procedure" with "full aftercare guidance"; custom crown, bridge or denture fitted
- "three and six months from placement to final restoration"; implants help prevent jawbone loss; 0% finance "subject to status, eligibility, and terms and conditions"

Smile makeover LP:
- "Straighten, brighten & enhance your smile"; "Fix chips, cracks & gaps - all under one roof"
- Invisalign "custom-crafted", "Bespoke retainer included"; combination plans "aligning your teeth with Invisalign before finishing with professional whitening and bonding"
- bonding: "Precisely colour-matched", "results achieved in just a single appointment", "without the need to cover the entire tooth"
- consultation: "advanced digital scanners and photography … a clear, honest picture of what is possible before you commit"; "transparent treatment plan"
- metal braces offered as well

Medical cards (client's own live ad copy in Motion; there is no LP): "now accepting Medical Card patients"; treatments listed are check-ups/examinations, cleaning or scale & polish, fillings, extractions. HSE entitlement as quoted on Boards.ie: "a dental examination, two fillings in each calendar year, extractions as necessary". The ads list examination, fillings and extractions only.

## Conflicts, so left off the ads

- **Prices.** Implants: LPs say from €1,400; the main site says from €1,800. Bonding: €180 (Artane LP) vs €200 (others). Metal braces: €3,100 (Artane/Balbriggan) vs €3,800 (others). Consultation €80 / €160 with OPG on the implant LP only. No prices on image or in copy.
- **Reviews.** Implant LPs say "Over 85+ Reviews", smile LPs "600+ Google Reviews", main site "500+". No review counts used; proof is "100% Irish owned, caring for families since 1980".
- **Staff.** The LP team lists are copied between clinics (e.g. Balbriggan's coordinator appears on the Kildare and Maynooth pages). No staff named.
- **Sedation.** The LPs offer IV and RA sedation, but our rule is no sedation language in ads aimed at nervous patients, so it's left out.

## Checks for the client

1. Are all three medical card clinics taking **new** medical card patients, and is scale & polish covered in their setup? The ads list exam, fillings and extractions only.
2. Which prices are correct (implants €1,400 vs €1,800, bonding €180 vs €200, braces €3,100 vs €3,800)? Prices can go on once confirmed.
3. Correct review count (85+, 500+ or 600+).
4. Team lists on the implant and smile LPs are copied between clinics and need fixing.
5. "Going Abroad" names no competitor or country; confirm the client is comfortable with the aftercare angle.
