### Four Steps

**Headlines:**
- Implants, explained step by step
- Dental implants in Balbriggan
- Know every step before you start

**Primary text:**

Not knowing what happens is half the worry with implants.

So here it is, step by step.

1. Consultation. You meet the team, talk about your goals and find out if implants suit you.
2. 3D scan and plan. X-rays and a CBCT scan show exactly where the implant should go, before anything is placed.
3. Placing the implant. A minor procedure, with full aftercare advice while it heals.
4. Your new tooth. Once it’s settled, your custom crown, bridge or denture is fitted.

From placement to the final tooth usually takes three to six months, and we explain every stage before it starts. 0% finance is available, subject to status and terms and conditions.

100% Irish owned, and caring for families since 1980.

Our Balbriggan clinic is at 6 Chapel Street, Balbriggan. Tap Book Now, leave your details, and the team will be in touch to arrange your implant consultation.

No surprises. Just a clear plan.
