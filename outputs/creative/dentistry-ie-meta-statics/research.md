## Persona bank (Dental Persona Bank, Shared Drive)

- **Mark, the lapsed patient (C1).** 35 to 55, last visit five or more years ago, triggered by a twinge that hasn't gone away. Expects to be judged for the gap, assumes the bill grows every year, doesn't know what happens at a first appointment. Casting: a warm clinician, not a senior or polished one.
- **Denise, switching (C2).** Wary of being upsold and has no idea what a check-up costs. "The practice manager is more credible than a clinician here." The Irish equivalent of "can't get an NHS place" is a practice that isn't taking new patients (see forum section).
- **Sophie, new to the area (C3).** 30 to 45, choosing on logistics and on whether she trusts the people.
