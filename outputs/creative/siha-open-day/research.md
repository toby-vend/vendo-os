## Market position

- **Sophistication:** stage 3 to 4. Local aligner ads lead with "straighten your smile", monthly finance and discounts. Siha's open day has two real mechanisms in its terms: **every plan is designed by a specialist orthodontist**, and you **see a 3D digital smile simulation before you commit**. Those lead; the £300 sits in the offer beat.
- **Awareness:** mostly solution aware (they know aligners exist) and problem aware (closed-lip photos, teeth that have moved).
- **Urgency is real:** one morning, 30-minute slots, and the price only applies to treatment paid for on the day.
