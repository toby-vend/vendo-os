## Notes: check with the practice before launch

1. **What's not in the £3,495.** A2 invites questions rather than naming exclusions. Confirm whether refinements, extra aligners and any rescans are included, so the ads can name them (the persona bank's strongest trust lever for Amy).
2. **Wear time.** The ads don't state hours. If Dr Syed gives a figure for 32Co (often 20 to 22 hours), it can be added to A5.
3. **"Clear aligners can treat crowded or overlapping teeth, gaps, and crooked or uneven teeth"** is from the landing page ("may be suitable for"). Confirm the practice is happy with this wording in ads.
4. Shared open day checks (package wording, eligibility, review count, follow-up speed) are in copy-open-day.md.
