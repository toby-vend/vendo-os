Who we are
A kind and caring approach

Oxford House is a family-orientated practice on Aylesbury Street in Fenny Stratford, in the south of Milton Keynes. From check-ups and hygiene to Invisalign, whitening, implants and facial aesthetics, the team takes pride in a gentle, caring approach.

It is also a referral centre. The team includes GDC-registered specialists in periodontics and endodontics and a specialist orthodontist, so complex work can be handled in-house rather than referred elsewhere.

The logo

A pencil sketch of the house itself over Oxford House in Teal, then the Kana Health Group crown on a hairline rule and DENTAL PRACTICE in Gold.

Clear space on every side equals the height of the crown. Use the stacked logo where there is room and the horizontal logo in tight spaces. Minimum width is 140px on screen, 35mm in print.

Full colour on white or Mist, white on Deep Teal or washed photography. The files are PNG only: crop from them, never redraw or retype.

06
Photography
Our team, our house

Real people in the real practice. The team in Oxford House teal scrubs, the navy lounge with its velvet chairs, the logo wall in reception, and the red-brick house that patients will recognise from the logo.

Use a person’s name only when it is confirmed against the website team page. This guideline names no one.

Look for
Warm eye contact and natural smiles, the navy lounge or bright surgeries softly behind. Bright, even light. Leave the left or top third quiet for a Deep Teal wash and headline.
Avoid
Generic stock families, instruments, needles, gloved hands in mouths, over-whitened teeth, and before-and-afters without the patient’s written consent.

07
Tone of voice
How we sound
Kind, family-centred and quietly expert, like a practice that has looked after the same families for decades. Patients should feel at ease and at home. UK English, no em dashes, no outcome guarantees or “pain-free” claims, no finance figures without FCA wording, and urgency only when an offer is genuinely live.
