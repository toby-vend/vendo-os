## Market position

- Sophistication: stage 3 to 4. Aligner ads locally lead with "straighten your smile", finance and before-and-afters. Oxford House's open day has a real mechanism in its terms: **one fixed price however complex the case**, when the market prices by tier. That's the lead.
- Awareness: mostly solution aware (they know aligners exist) and problem aware (closed-lip photos).
- Proof gap: no aligner-specific reviews and no consented aligner before-and-afters. The LP's before-and-after image is not an aligner case (and carries another dentist's watermark), so it is not used.
