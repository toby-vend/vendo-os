02
Logo
The logo

Wide-spaced Montserrat capitals with the line-drawn tooth mark. Three lock-ups: horizontal (tooth between the words), stacked (DENTAL STUDIOS in bold) and the round badge.

Clear space on every side equals the height of the tooth mark. Minimum width: horizontal 240px, stacked 140px, badge 70px.

Ink on light grounds, white on Navy or dark photos. The tooth alone is an icon or avatar. Never redraw, retype or re-space it.

05
Graphic elements
The village house

The brand sheet builds its palette, icons and pattern from one shape: a simple gabled house. It is the "village" in Cheam Village, and the signature frame for photos.

Use one house-framed photo per layout, with a gable roughly a quarter to a third of its width. The trio of houses and the double slash are small accents. The mosaic circle is a quiet background pattern.

No drop shadows, no glow, no tilted cards, no boxes round copy.

06
Photography
Our practice, our people

The practice's own shoot: the navy feature wall with the round logo, slatted oak, soft plants and fresh flowers, the team in black scrubs, the iTero scanner in use, and the shopfront on Upper Mulgrave Road.

Name a team member only when the website confirms the name and role, and only when the face in the photo is theirs.

Look for
Natural light, navy and oak, real people mid-conversation, the scanner screen, calm waiting room details. Patients who look relaxed, not posed.
Avoid
Stock models, harsh clinical white, needles and drills, gloved hands in mouths, and before-and-afters without the patient's written consent.

Tone of voice
How we sound
Warm, personal and quietly expert, like a family practice that knows you by name. UK English, sentence case in body copy, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no outcome guarantees or "pain-free" claims, no finance figures without FCA wording, and urgency only when an offer is genuinely live.
