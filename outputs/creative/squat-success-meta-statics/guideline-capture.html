Who we are
Opening your own practice shouldn’t be a leap of faith.

Squat Success helps UK dentists open a squat: a brand-new practice built from scratch, rather than bought.

It was founded by Dr Bobby Bhandal, who left corporate dentistry and built Avenue Dental in Leamington Spa from an empty unit. His book, The Dental Freedom Blueprint, sets out the system he used. The programme gives dentists who have already decided a sequenced plan, direct access to Bobby and a cohort of peers, and accountability.

02
Logo
The logo

A gold and white SS monogram beside SQUAT SUCCESS in a high-contrast serif. The second S carries the gold gradient; never flatten or recolour it.

Clear space on every side equals the width of one S. Minimum width is 120px on screen, 30mm in print.

The monogram works alone as an avatar, favicon and stamp. In ads the lockup sits top left or top centre, white, about a quarter of the canvas width.

06
Photography
Real dentists, real rooms.

Low-key and warm: dark grounds, tungsten light, shallow depth of field. Three families of shot: Bobby on stage and in the practice; members telling their story on camera; and the book itself, held, posted and read.

Use Squat Success’s own photography and event footage. Members appear only with their consent and are named the way they are on the site.

Avoid
Bright clinical stock, generic handshakes and skylines, smiling-patient imagery (the audience is dentists, not patients), and anything that suggests a get-rich-quick lifestyle.

Tone of voice
How we sound
Like Bobby across the table over a cup of tea: conversational, British, a little self-deprecating, and straight about how hard it is. We talk to dentists as peers. UK English, no em dashes, no hype, no false scarcity. The book is printed to order, so we never say copies are running out.
