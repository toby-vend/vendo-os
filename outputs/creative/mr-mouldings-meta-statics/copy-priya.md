## P4 | Proper scale
**Awareness:** problem aware. **Sophistication:** 4. **Angle:** stock profiles are scaled down; period rooms need period proportions.
Research: "rather than scaled down and simplified for today's tiny box houses" (Period Property forum, mardinor); "Reproductions tend to be scaled down and often simplify details to make them easier to machine." (Period Property forum, fernicarry); FAQ: 167mm to 219mm suits Victorian and Edwardian rooms.
Concept name: **Priya Period Range | Proper scale**
Talent: Room photo

**On image:** Period rooms need period proportions. / 167mm to 219mm skirting for Victorian and Edwardian ceilings. Torus, ogee, bolection, Georgian, Regency. / Shop Victorian skirting

**Headline:** Skirting that doesn't look lost.

**Primary text:**
A three-metre ceiling with a 119mm skirting looks like the room has shrunk at the bottom.

Most stock profiles are scaled down for modern houses. The detail gets simplified, the height comes down, and in a period room it looks lost.

Victorian and Edwardian rooms with taller ceilings want 167mm to 219mm skirting, and a thickness that clears the architrave without a step. 22mm gives that heavier, traditional reveal.

We machine period profiles at period sizes: torus, ogee, bolection, Georgian, Regency, lambs tongue, with architrave, dado and picture rail to match. Over 150 profiles in the standard range, and anything else matched from a sample.

Made in our Epsom workshop since 2003.

Shop Victorian skirting, or send a sample if the house has its own profile.

Get the proportions right and the room looks like itself again.
