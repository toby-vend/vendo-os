## PART A: MR Mouldings' own reputation

### READ FIRST: most of the on-site review count looks generated

The site says "Rated 4.6 out of 5 / From 11,700+ product reviews" (pdp trust card on https://www.mdfskirtingmouldings.co.uk/products/ogee-2-mdf-skirting-board). Those reviews are served by **Yotpo** (app key in page source; public widget endpoint `https://api-cdn.yotpo.com/v1/widget/1z1soahRJHY0Ag8JFaDzCsfW4RHd6DRf9RRgqUW3/products/<shopify product id>/reviews.json`). I pulled every review across all 723 products plus the shop-level feed: **11,743 reviews, average 4.57.**

What the data shows:
- **10,608 of 11,743 (90%) are built entirely from a pool of ~200 stock sentences**, recombined. E.g. "Looks far more expensive than it is." appears 1,013 times; "Only minor sanding needed before painting." 861 times; "Brings character back into the property." 702 times; "Used this on a client project recently." 606 times. The same three-sentence review appears word for word up to 18 times.
- Those templated reviews are **only ever 4 or 5 stars**, and cluster Aug 2025 to Feb 2026 (369 in Aug 2025, then 1,300 to 1,800 a month), versus roughly 10 to 50 a month of free-text reviews.
- A further ~800 use a second template ("We fitted the edwardian panel mould in our hallway and dining room and the result is excellent...", "(Order ref: DR1383)" suffixes), with the same reviewer name/date/text posted up to 4 times.
- Other bulk blocks: ~100 two-sentence reviews with sequential IDs 7965749xx to 7965751xx (Oct 2025 to Jan 2026, "staff appologised and sorted it" style); a 2024 batch posted twice each (IDs 6749892xx/6748019xx); and a 2026 block (IDs 89502xxxx) where title and body are identical marketing headlines ("Premium Astragal Moulding, Quickly Delivered").
- One shop-level review is about a DFS sofa ("Sofa was delivered on time as promised by DFS", Thomas E., 5 July 2026), so imported/irrelevant reviews are also in the feed.
- **Only ~300 to 340 reviews read as genuine free text.** Among those, the 1 to 3 star share is much higher (33 of 339) and the recurring complaints are delivery time/cost and rough primer.

**Implication for ads:** do not use "11,700+ reviews" or "4.6 from 11,700+" in Meta copy, and do not quote any templated review. Under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (fake review rules in force since 6 April 2025), publishing or relying on fake/unverified reviews is a banned practice. This needs raising with the client. Trustpilot (below) is the safer public proof point. Only the reviews quoted below (specific, idiosyncratic, mixed-sentiment) should be treated as real customer voice, and even these come from a feed the client controls.
