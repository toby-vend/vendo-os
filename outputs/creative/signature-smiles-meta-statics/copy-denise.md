# Denise: no NHS place | Meta copy

Campaign: General. Persona: Denise, 40 to 70. Her practice went private or she can't get registered anywhere. She assumes private means upselling and has no idea whether a check-up is £40 or £150 (Dental Persona Bank, General Dentistry mini). The fee said out loud, with the number on screen, is the whole brief. Offer across all five: new patient exam £90 including two x-rays; optional £20 monthly plan. Research: research.md (Denise section, offer facts). Location line: Brackley.

Casting: women 45 to 70, warm and ordinary; the practice manager or reception is more credible than a clinician for this persona.

## Notes: check with the client before launch
- **Plan discount:** the site says 10% off further treatment in two places and 20% in the FAQ. The ads leave the discount out. Ask Chirali which is right.
- **"You don't have to join a plan to be seen"** (D2, D4): the site lists pay-as-you-go fees, so this follows, but confirm.
- **"Nothing starts until you've seen the price and said yes"** (D1): the site says "you'll always know the cost of your treatment before it begins". Confirm they're comfortable with the stronger wording.
- **"We'll tell you the price up front"** (D5) refers to the published £90 exam fee and price list.
- Never name or compare with NHS practices or the local competitors.
