## Notes: checks for the client before launch

1. **What the £95 includes** is worded three ways on the site (/fees-finance/: one x-ray; /emergency-care/ FAQ: full assessment and pain relief; /new-patients/: x-ray, assessment and advice). The ads say only "includes an x-ray". Primary text says "find the cause, deal with/ease the pain": confirm pain relief is part of the £95 visit, or cut that line.
2. **Same day:** the ads always say "where possible" or "where we can", matching the site. Confirm the practice is happy to run emergency ads at all (they need free slots to back them).
3. **Booking route:** emergency booking is by phone (01280 733343), so these ads suit a Call Now button. If the General campaign is lead-form only, the CTA needs changing to "Leave your number and we'll call you back".
4. **Out of hours:** the site states no out-of-hours service, so nothing in the ads implies one. "Call first thing" covers late-night viewers.
5. **Rating:** the site's Google widget shows five stars from 112 reviews; recheck the exact average on Google Maps before launch (the copy uses the widget's own wording, "Excellent", and no decimal).
6. **Photography:** client's own only. R3 shows and names Lorraine Murphy (Front of House, /about-us/): confirm she's happy to appear in ads. No words are put in her mouth.
7. **Shot list for the next shoot (Ray):** reception phone being answered (hand on handset, desk, orchid); a treatment room ready for an emergency visit; Lorraine at the desk on the phone; the front door with opening hours visible.
