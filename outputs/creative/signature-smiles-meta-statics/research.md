## Market position (Brackley)

- Nearby on Google: Dentalcare Brackley 4.1 (105), Bupa Dental Care Brackley 4.6 (78), South Gables 4.7 (65). Signature Smiles is the only 5.0 with 100+ reviews in the local list.
- Sophistication: stage 2 to 3 for general dentistry locally ("new patients welcome", "family dentist"). The edges that pass the three tests (a proposition, unavailable to competitors, would move a stranger): **the price said out loud**, **5.0 from 110**, **longer appointments with a limited list**. For aligners: **every case planned by a specialist orthodontist**, and **retainers included in the price**.
- What wins for this account (28 Aug static wash-up): short, specific or location-led hooks ("When was your last dental checkup?" worked; long generic hooks lost). Women 35 to 44 are the strongest audience on the General campaign (22 May call).
