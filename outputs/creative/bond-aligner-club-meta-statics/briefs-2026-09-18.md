## Fixed rules (from the deck and client feedback)

**Palette:** Bond Gold #D2B78C, Bond Black #0A0A0A and Cream #FAF3E9. Nothing else. Gold is used either as a background with black on top, or as text on black.

**Logo:**
- Use the full Bond Aligner Club lockup, never redrawn.
- Keep clear space equal to at least the height of the mark.
- Show "by Bond Dental London" on every creative.

**Tagline:** "Start as you mean to go on." Use it as-is wherever possible.

**Pricing, verbatim:**

| Tier | Price | Aligners |
|---|---|---|
| Express | £995 | Up to 7 |
| Lite | £1,995 | Up to 14 |
| Moderate | £2,995 | Up to 20 |
| Comprehensive | £3,595 | Unlimited |

0% finance is available on all tiers over up to 36 months.

**Never:**
- Hard sell, urgency, or discount or budget framing
- "Best", "most popular" or "Apex"
- Implying guaranteed life outcomes
- Em dashes
- The old Marylebone clinic photo
- AI-generated people

**Education:** weave in genuine educational content (why alignment matters, what treatment involves) as well as the humour.

**Tone:** confident, a little wry, self-aware "main character" humour, warm and calm.
