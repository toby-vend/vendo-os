# Bond Aligner Club: sourced research quote bank (raw)

Compiled 3 October 2026 for Meta ad copy. Every quote below is verbatim, copied from the page text (spelling, typos and punctuation kept as posted). Excerpts are contiguous runs of the original post. Where a post is longer, only the quoted run is used; nothing has been reworded.

**Usage rules**
- Forum quotes are marked "forum, not a Bond patient". They are voice-of-customer research for angles and wording. They must not be presented in ads as Bond testimonials.
- Bond review quotes are marked "Bond patient review (public)". Using a named review, or a reviewer's words as a quote, in an ad needs the patient's permission (and Bond's sign-off).
- Mumsnet links use the post permalink format `?reply=<post id>` taken from each post's share link. Thread-opening posts link to the thread URL.
- Dates are the post date as shown on the page (DD/MM/YYYY). Google review dates are relative ("3 months ago") as displayed on 3 October 2026.

**Coverage notes**
- Reddit skipped (blocked).
- Mumsnet: 24 threads read in full. MoneySavingExpert forum: 2 threads read.
- Netmums, Patient.info and Which?: searches returned no usable UK forum threads on this topic (results were clinic blogs), so nothing is quoted from them.
- Trustpilot: blocked by a bot-verification page; not bypassed. No Trustpilot data.
- Google Maps per-review topic filtering (Invisalign chip) would not respond in the browser, so Bond review quotes come from the Google reviews widget embedded on the BAC landing page and the reviews on bonddental.co.uk/testimonials/. Star ratings and counts per clinic come from Google Maps directly.
- Persona D ("Dan") is male; Mumsnet posters are mostly women. The relapse quotes are still on-message (braces as a teen, retainer abandoned, teeth moved back) but none are from a man in his own words.

Abbreviations in the "type" column: F = forum, not a Bond patient. MSE = MoneySavingExpert forum, not a Bond patient.

## Conflicts and checks before copy goes live

1. **Clinic count: 4 vs 5.** LP says "4 London clinics" (Marylebone, Mayfair, King's Cross, Notting Hill). Main site, Kev's bio and Google Maps list a fifth, Kensington (151 North End Road, W14 9NH). Ads should say four, or confirm whether Kensington offers BAC.
2. **Consultation and scan: free vs £50.** LP: "Free consultation & 3D scan". Main site Invisalign page lists the scan as "worth £50" inside the package, and the fee guide charges "Investigations (unlimited Intraoral X-rays, scans, and photos per course of treatment) £50.00". The Invisalign consultation itself is "FREE" in the fee guide. No £50 deposit wording found anywhere. Confirm with Bond that X-rays and scan are free for BAC consultations before saying "free scan".
3. **Whitening.** Main site: whitening "worth £595" is included with Invisalign "from £995". BAC: whitening only on Moderate (£2,995) and Comprehensive (£3,595). Express and Lite buyers who have seen the main site may expect it. (The main site also sells Bond Whitening at £395, so "worth £595" and £395 sit side by side.)
4. **Retainers.** Main site: "Free Removable Retainers (worth £395 per arch)" and the fee guide's Vivera line "3 sets included (Per arch) £395.00". BAC: "One set of complimentary retainers" and FAQ "one complimentary removable retainer". Avoid quoting a retainer value in BAC ads.
5. **Refinements: Comprehensive only, or every tier?** LP tier card says Comprehensive is "refinements included", which reads as exclusive. The FAQ says clinician-deemed refinements are "part of your treatment on every tier" and the price is "A fixed amount". This is BAC's strongest answer to Persona B's hidden-cost fear, so confirm the every-tier wording with Bond and make the tier card match.
6. **Diamond Provider.** Claimed on the Marylebone, Mayfair and Kensington Invisalign pages; absent from the LP. Not verified against Invisalign's own provider finder. Forum researchers do look for this tier (Persona B quote above). Verify the current tier before using it in ads.
7. **Monthly figure rounding.** £3,595 over 36 months is £99.86, shown as "~£99". The other three tiers round to the nearest pound (£27.64 to ~£28, £55.42 to ~£55, £83.19 to ~£83). Consider "~£100" for Comprehensive.
8. **Review counts.** LP trust bar "1,000+" and widget "1,031 reviews"; Google Maps on 3 October 2026 shows 1,278 across five listings (1,204 across the four BAC clinics). "1,000+" and "4.8" are both safe.
9. **Wear time.** LP "Around 22 hours a day"; main site "20–22 hours". Minor.
10. **Address details in the FCA footer.** It lists "96 Marchmont Street, London, W11 3HL" (W11 3HL is the Notting Hill postcode; King's Cross is WC1N 1AG) and "109 Gloucester Place, Marylebone, London, W1U 8HW" (elsewhere W1U 6JW). Flag to Bond; does not affect ad copy.
11. **King's Cross naming.** LP labels it King's Cross but "Near Russell Square"; one Google review calls it the "Bloomsbury Clinic". Use "King's Cross" consistently in ads.
