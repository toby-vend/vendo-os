# The Aspirational Professional | Meta copy

**Campaign:** Bond Aligner Club (Meta).

**Persona:** women 25 to 44, strongest at 25 to 34 (early Meta data, about two weeks). London professionals who follow trends and want it to feel aspirational, but not out of reach. Source: Bond calls of 9 Sept and 1 Oct.

**Offer across all five:**
- Genuine Invisalign at four clear prices: £995, £1,995, £2,995 and £3,595.
- 0% finance available.
- Consultation and 3D scan, where you see your plan and price before you commit.

**Messaging:**
- Kev's pillars: Quality, Premium, Affordable. "Your values aligned with ours."
- Copy says "premium Central London clinics"; no clinic is named.
- Targeting: 3 to 5 miles around Marylebone.

**Research:** research-raw.md, Persona A. Forum quotes are voice-of-customer only, never presented as Bond patients.

Each ad's image, headline and primary text start from the same awareness stage. The static carries the hook, problem, promise and mechanism. The primary text carries the rest of the nine beats.

## Notes: confirm with Bond before launch

- **Consultation wording.** The landing page says "Free consultation & 3D scan". The fee guide charges £50 for scans, and the 1 Oct call mentioned a £50 refundable deposit. The copy says "Book a consultation" and never "free" until this is confirmed.
- **Review figures.** "4.8★ from 1,000+ Google reviews" matches the landing page's trust bar. Google Maps showed 1,278 reviews across five clinics on 3 Oct. Named reviews aren't used.
- **Finance.** "0% finance available, subject to status" only, with no monthly figures (the lender's illustration and FCA wording are still pending). Add the credit-broker line in the ad if Meta asks: KEVOM LTD t/a Bond Dental London, FRN 832135.
- **Whitening.** Whitening is only included on Moderate and Comprehensive, per the landing page. The main site says it's included from £995, which conflicts.
- **"People rarely notice" (P2).** This is based on forum experiences, not a Bond claim. Kev may prefer "clear and removable" only.
- **Visits and timings (P2).** "At least three visits" and "Express and Lite cases are typically a few months" come from the landing page FAQ.
- **10% off (P5).** 10% off general treatments for members, as Toby confirmed on 3 Oct. The landing page says "general and cosmetic", which needs aligning.
- **Imagery.** Photos are Bond's own shoot. Models in the shoot need image-use consent for paid ads.
