# Sophie: family register | Woburn Sands | Meta copy

Campaign: General (new patient), Meta, £500/month. Persona: Sophie, 30 to 45, two or three children, new to the area, choosing a practice on logistics and on whether she trusts the people (Dental Persona Bank, General Dentistry mini). Offer: new patient check-up £85 for adults (X-rays included), £25 for a child's new patient assessment (fees page). Target area: Woburn Sands and the villages around it (Aspley Guise, Bow Brickhill, Wavendon, per the guideline's catchment note; set in the ad set). Copy names Woburn Sands only. Research: research.md.

Each ad's image, headline and primary text start from the same awareness stage. Statics carry hook, problem, promise and mechanism; the primary text carries the rest. CTA wording: Book Appointment. No plan prices (conflicting on the membership page), no Saturday hours, no parking claim, no named reviews, no before-and-afters.

Ad names (build standard): `General | Static | Sophie | <Detail> | <1x1 / 9x16 / 1.91x1> | 261027`.
