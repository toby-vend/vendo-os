# Denise: no NHS place | Wilson House | Meta copy

Campaign: General (new patient), Meta, £500/month. Persona: Denise, 40 to 70, can't get an NHS place, assumes private means upselling, has no idea what a private check-up costs (Dental Persona Bank, General Dentistry mini). Offer on every ad: new patient check-up £85 with X-rays. Target area: Newport Pagnell and the villages around it (practice's own town; set the radius in the ad set, copy names Newport Pagnell only). Research: research.md.

Each ad's image, headline and primary text start from the same awareness stage. Statics carry hook, problem, promise and mechanism; the primary text carries the rest of the nine beats. CTA wording: Book Appointment (LP SOP). No sedation, finance, before-and-afters or named reviews.

Ad names (build standard): `General | Static | Denise | <Detail> | <1x1 / 9x16 / 1.91x1> | 261027`.
