Who we are
Caring for every smile in Newport Pagnell

Wilson House is a private practice on Station Road in Newport Pagnell, in the red-brick house its logo is drawn from. It offers check-ups and hygiene, fillings, crowns and bridges, implants and dentures, whitening and bonding, Invisalign and children’s dentistry.

Many patients have been coming for decades and now bring their children and grandchildren. The town is settled and family-minded: people choose a dentist they can trust for years, who explains things clearly and makes nervous patients feel at ease.

02
Logo
The logo

A hand-drawn sketch of the practice building, stepped gable and all, above Wilson House in a Purple serif. Beneath sit the Kana Health Group crown on a fine rule, then DENTAL PRACTICE in Gold.

Clear space on every side equals the height of the crown. Minimum width is 120px on screen, 30mm in print.

The house sketch can stand alone as a watermark. The crown alone is a bullet icon. Use the full-colour logo on white or Cream and the white logo on Purple or washed photography. The files are PNG only: crop from them, never redraw or retype.

06
Photography
Our team, our house

Real people in the real practice. The team in purple scrubs, bright surgeries with tall sash windows, the mustard chairs of the waiting room, and the red-brick house on Station Road that the logo is drawn from.

Name a team member only when the name is confirmed on the website team page. Never caption anyone as a specific dentist on a guess.

Look for
Warm eye contact and natural smiles. Purple scrubs, soft window light, the stepped gable and blossom outside. Leave the left or top third quiet for a Purple wash and headline.
Avoid
Stock models, instruments, needles, gloved hands in mouths, over-whitened teeth, and before-and-afters without the patient's written consent.

07
Tone of voice
How we sound
Friendly, family-centred and clear, like a dentist the whole family has known for years. Patients should always know what happens next. UK English, no em dashes, no outcome guarantees or “pain-free” claims, no finance figures without FCA wording, and urgency only when an offer is genuinely live.
Reassuring
We say
We’ll take time to explain each step.
We don’t say
Pain-free dentistry, guaranteed!
Honest
We say
Clear advice and a care plan built around you.
We don’t say
The best dentist in Milton Keynes.
