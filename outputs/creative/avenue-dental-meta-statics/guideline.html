Who we are
Who we are
Rooted in Leamington Spa. Built for the community.

Avenue is a private practice on Lime Avenue in Lillington, founded by Dr Bobby Bhandal, who grew up around the corner. It covers check-ups and hygiene, implants and dentures, Invisalign and orthodontics, bonding, veneers and whitening, and children's dentistry.

The idea was simple: a dental visit that feels less clinical and more like home. The lounge has velvet chairs, marble and warm wood, the team takes its time, and the practice was Highly Commended for its care of nervous patients.

Logo
The logo

A tall Didone-style AP monogram in Main Gold, with AVENUE DENTAL set in widely spaced capitals across its middle.

Clear space on every side equals the height of the wordmark capitals times three. Minimum width is 110px on screen, 25mm in print.

Use the black wordmark on white or Blush Gold and the white wordmark on Black or dark photography. The monogram alone works as a watermark, an avatar and the round black sign in the practice. Never redraw or retype it.

The site navigation bar; use sparingly
Avenue is dark-first: Black carries most ads and sections, as a solid ground or a deep wash over real practice photography, with White text and one Main Gold phrase per headline. Roughly 60% Black, 20% White, 12% Blush Gold, the rest gold. Main Gold is for the monogram, pill buttons, rules and the accent phrase; Sand Gold lifts highlights; Blush Gold is the light alternative ground. Body text on Black is a soft off-white (#D9D4CC). No clinical blues, no bright colours, no gold on gold.

05
Shapes and frames
One soft corner

On the website, photos are square-cornered rectangles with one large rounded corner, usually the one facing the copy. That single curve is the signature: calm and modern, never bubbly.

Line icons sit inside an open gold ring with a small gap. Buttons are full gold pills with black text. Circles are kept for the monogram sign and avatars.

No drop shadows, no glow, no tilted cards, no hard boxes round copy.

07
Photography
Our practice, our people

Real people in the real practice: the team in black scrubs, the lounge with its velvet chairs, marble and slatted wood, the round gold-on-black sign, and the red-brick corner building on Lime Avenue, lit up at night.

Name a team member only when the name is confirmed on the website. The founder, Dr Bobby Bhandal, is named on the site.

Look for
Warm low light, dark tones and gold details. Calm conversations in the lounge, the team at work in black scrubs, the building at dusk. Patients who look relaxed, not posed.
Avoid
Stock models, bright clinical white rooms, instruments and needles, gloved hands in mouths, and before-and-afters without the patient's written consent.

08
Tone of voice
How we sound
Confident and warm, like a trusted local expert. Never salesy, never clinical. UK English, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no outcome guarantees or “pain-free” claims, no finance figures without FCA wording, and urgency only when an offer is genuinely live.
