# Mark: the lapsed patient | Meta copy

Campaign: VD | General (instant form). Persona: Mark, 35 to 55, last visit five or more years ago, triggered by a twinge that hasn't gone away. He expects to be judged for the gap, assumes the bill grows with every year, doesn't know what a first appointment involves, and is waiting to feel ready (Dental Persona Bank). The 15 Oct call adds that General leads are often new to the area and booking new patient exams.

Offer across all five: a new patient appointment: detailed examination, digital X-rays if needed, oral cancer screening, a 3D scan, a personalised care plan and "time to ask questions" (lp.avenuedentalpractice.co.uk/new-patients-offer/). Proof: 4.9 from 227 Google reviews; Private Dentistry Awards 2025, Highly Commended for Treatment of Nervous Patients (Midlands). Research: research.md (Mark).

Rules for Mark: shame closes wallets, so no line implies he's let himself go. The cost of waiting is practical, never a telling off. No exam price on the image until Avenue confirms £49 or £75. No sedation wording. Warm, unpolished; close to an empty waiting room, never busy. CTA describes the instant form: short form, the team calls back.
