## New conflicts and checks (flag to the client)

- **Implant-retained dentures wording:** the card says "Same day, high quality & aesthetic fixed solution" and "The most cost effective treatment for permanently replacing a whole arch". Implant-retained dentures are usually removable (they clip on). Ads say "held in place by implants" and never "fixed" or "same day" until Avenue confirms which product £8,100 buys and how many implants it uses.
- **Free consultation for dentures:** the dentures page has "Book Your Free Consultation" buttons but no "worth £250" line (that is on the implants page). Ads say "free consultation" without a worth value.
- **Shade choice:** the site says shade matching and colour-matched bonding. It does not say the patient chooses the shade. Hannah ads say "matched to your own teeth", not "you choose it", until confirmed.
- **Review count:** landing pages show 150+, 160+, 180+ and 221 against 227 on the homepage. Worth aligning before the VC campaigns send traffic there.
- Batch 1 conflicts (exam price, "Save £1,125" with no end date, £14,500 arch wording, bonding lifespan, before-and-after consent) still stand.
