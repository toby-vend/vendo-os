## Notes: open items for the client
- **What £8,100 buys:** the card calls implant-retained dentures a "Same day ... fixed solution" for "permanently replacing a whole arch". Implant-retained dentures usually clip on and come out for cleaning. Confirm whether £8,100 is a removable implant-retained denture or a fixed bridge, and how many implants it uses. The ads say only "held in place by implants".
- **Free consultation for dentures:** the dentures page has "Book Your Free Consultation" buttons but no "worth £250" line. Confirm the consultation is free for denture enquiries too.
- L3: "age on its own isn't a reason to rule them out" is general clinical guidance. Confirm Avenue's clinicians are happy with the line.
- Dentures from £1,045 (partial) and £1,320 (full): confirm these are current. L2 depends on them.
- The 12-month guarantee is listed for the implant-retained denture only; L2 doesn't attach it to the other tiers.
- L2 shows an Avenue dentist and a patient from the Drive shoot. Confirm the patient consented to paid ads, and that the ad does not imply he is a denture patient (the copy addresses the viewer, not him).
- The menu and drawing are illustrations.
- Imagery gap: no Drive photos of patients aged 60 to 75. Persona bank says real patients only for Linda. Shot list: a denture or implant-retained denture patient (consented) eating at home or at a table; hands holding the actual denture model in the surgery; the dentist explaining with the model.
