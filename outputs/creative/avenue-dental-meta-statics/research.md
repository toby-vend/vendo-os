## Conflicts (left off the ads, flagged to the client)

- New patient exam: the landing page shows £49 and £75 side by side with a note that "The £49 fee covers the emergency consultation only"; the fee guide says £75; the Figma variables sheet says £49; the April design brief says £75. No exam price goes on the ads until confirmed.
- Single implant: landing page £2,450; Figma variables sheet £1,250. Ads use £2,450 (the page the ads link to).
- "Single Arch £14,500" is described on the same card as replacing "both arches". Confirm what £14,500 buys.
- "Save over £1125 ... limited time only" (implants) and "Save £1000 – limited time offer" (main implants page): no end date given. No urgency on the ads until Avenue confirms an end date.
- Bonding lifespan: "5–7+ years" (top of page) vs "4 to 8 years" (FAQ).
- Clear aligners from £2,500 (landing page) vs Invisalign from £3,750 (Figma variables). Not used: no aligner persona this round.
- Before-and-afters: the smile makeover page says its photos are "shared with their permission". Confirm that consent covers paid ads before any appear in one.
- Implant lifespan: landing page "can last more than 25 years"; main page "15–20 years or more". Not used.

### Mark (lapsed 5+ years)
- Accommodation: "Through the 20 years I didn't go I became more and more embarrassed of my crooked teeth and as a result I never smile or laugh with them showing" [M3]
- The word / nearly didn't: "Most of the reason I can't bring myself to go is shame/embarrassment rather than fear at this point tbh" [M5]; "I'm so anxious about what they're going to say and I just feel like they're going to humiliate me and tell me off" [M1]
- Failed solution: "My last dentist was so unkind and often scolded me for my teeth." [M8]
- Moment: "when I finally plucked up the courage to go I was embarrassed to admit how long it had been … he didn't judge me" [M9]
- After: "I need fillings but nowhere near as bad or expensive as I thought." [M6]
- Patterns: "plucked up the courage"; shame outranks fear; it gets harder the longer it's left.
