The assessment

More than a check-up. A proper look at your oral health.

We take time to understand your short- and long-term goals, then tailor your plan to them. Small issues are fixed early, before they get worse: prevention before cure.

Your oral health is part of your overall health

Gum disease is linked to chronic conditions including cardiovascular disease, diabetes and Alzheimer’s. That’s why your assessment covers teeth, gums, bite and jaw, an oral cancer screening, small X-rays and a 3D scan, not just a quick look.
