04 · Buttons, tags & shape
Everything rounded.
Sign Up Talk To Our Team Learn More ↗
Primary = gradient. Secondary = ink. Tertiary = outline. One primary per view. ↗ marks navigation, → marks an action.
General Employers +£36.99 60 seconds
Tags: label colour at ~10% tint behind, fully rounded, caps. Green is for money gains only.
Cards 24–28px radius. Inputs 14px with a 1.5px hairline and magenta focus ring. Buttons full pill. No sharp corners. Shadows no heavier than a whisper.
05 · Photography
Life being lived.
Real people, candid, warm ambient light. Never desks or graphs. The gradient does the fintech; photos do the humanity.
06 · Voice
Like a friend would.
Plain English, second person, lightly cheeky: “You might be richer than you think.” Benefit-first headlines of 3–6 words. Warm but never flippant about money.
07 · Meta ads
Lit grounds. One idea per ad.

Callouts. The question is the whole message. No highlight, no supporting line.
Artefacts are illustrative mock-ups built in type. Never a provider's screen, name, logo or colour.

09 · Financial promotions
Every ad carries the risk.
Risk panel · build from these lines
Always: Capital at risk. The value of investments can go down as well as up and members may get back less than is paid in.
Add as relevant: Tax rules can change. · Compound does not provide financial advice. · Choice of workplace pension scheme remains the employer's decision. · Compound links to the accounting software you already use, not to every system.
Paid placement: Compound Digital Ltd is an appointed representative of RiskSave Technologies Ltd, which is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 775330).
RiskSave signs off every ad
• No comparison with a named provider, stated or implied.
• Any figure (time saved, returns, pot values) needs evidence on file and its source.
• Mock-ups must read as illustrative: “Example”, “Draft”, “Unsent”. Never a real record or quote.
• Put the premise as a question, not an assertion about the reader.
• No unconditional promises (“forever”) or primacy claims.
• A batch only goes live once every item in it is approved or withdrawn.
