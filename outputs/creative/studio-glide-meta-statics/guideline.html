05 · Photography
Real people, real daylight

Natural daylight, cream walls, pale wood and black reformer upholstery. Real clients and instructors mid-movement or mid-laugh.

Do use the studio’s own shoots, keep grades warm and soft, crop close on movement.

Don’t use stock poses, dark moody grades, heavy filters, or text over faces.
