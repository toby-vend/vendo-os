# Landing Page Brief: Studio Glide Pilates, Oxted Founding Membership

**Client:** Studio Glide Pilates
**Project:** Oxted launch landing page
**Prepared by:** Vendo Digital
**Date:** 30 July 2026

---

## 1. Objective

**Primary goal:** Capture qualified local leads onto the Oxted priority list so they can enter an automated nurture flow.

**Secondary goal:** Convert the highest intent visitors directly into one of 10 Founding Memberships (£2,000 for 200 credits).

**Why in this order:** The offer is capped at 10 units, so maximum direct revenue is £20,000. That is not a scalable paid social objective. The offer's real job is to generate attention, urgency and a reason to hand over an email address before the studio opens. Every lead captured now is a member acquired at opening.

**Primary KPI:** Cost per lead (priority list signups)
**Secondary KPI:** Founding Memberships sold
**Tertiary KPI:** Lead to member conversion rate at opening

## 3. Strategic Recommendation

### 3.1 Dual conversion path

The page needs two clearly differentiated actions, not one.

**Path A, primary (low friction, high volume):**
Join the Oxted priority list. Name, email, mobile. Feeds the marketing flow. This is where 95%+ of traffic will convert.

**Path B, secondary (high friction, high value):**
Claim a Founding Membership. Direct to Momence checkout. Visible above the fold and repeated after the value stack, but visually secondary to Path A in weight, not in prominence.

### 3.2 The sold out problem

Once 10 memberships are gone, the page's headline offer is dead but the traffic is not. Build the page with a **switchable state** from day one:

- **State 1 (live):** Founding Membership available, X of 10 remaining
- **State 2 (sold out):** "All 10 Founding Memberships have gone." Priority list becomes the sole CTA, with a stated benefit: early access booking and a founder rate offer at opening

This should be a single toggle in the CMS, not a rebuild.

### 3.3 Scarcity handling

Real scarcity is the strongest asset here. Use a visible counter ("7 of 10 remaining") and update it manually. Do not fake it or use a fake countdown timer. Meta ad policy and brand trust both suffer, and in a town the size of Oxted word travels.

## 4. Audience and Messaging

### 4.1 Primary audience

Women, 28 to 55, within roughly 15 minutes' drive of Oxted (Oxted, Limpsfield, Godstone, Westerham, Edenbridge, Caterham, Hurst Green, Tandridge). Household income above average for the region. Already Pilates aware or actively considering it.

Three sub-segments worth separating in ad creative and in the post-signup survey:

1. **The committed regular.** Already trains 3 to 4 times a week, likely already paying a competitor. The founding offer is a straight economic argument.
2. **The curious starter.** Wants to try Pilates, intimidated by reformer machines, put off by price. Needs reassurance more than discount.
3. **The returner.** Post-injury, post-baby, or coming back after a break. The Mama Method and small class sizes are the hook.

### 4.2 Core message hierarchy

1. Studio Glide is opening in Oxted
2. 10 people will get 200 classes for £2,000, and no one will ever get this price again
3. That is £10 a class across Reformer, Mat, Barre and Yoga
4. Everyone else can join the priority list for early access and opening updates

### 4.3 Tone

Warm, confident, understated premium. Not shouty discount language. The scarcity does the urgency work, the copy does not need to.

Avoid: body transformation claims, before and after framing, weight loss promises, anything implying the reader has a personal attribute or problem. This is both a brand fit issue and a Meta ad policy issue (personal attributes and health and wellness restrictions).

## 7. Tech, Tracking and Build

### 7.1 Hosting

**[Decision needed]** Two options:

- **Squarespace page on studioglidepilates.com/oxted-founding.** Fastest to ship, consistent domain, inherits existing GTM container (GTM-TMPMWCSX). Limited layout control and slower.
- **Standalone build.** Better performance and control, but adds a domain and a tracking setup.

**Recommendation:** Squarespace, on the existing domain, unless conversion rate testing is a priority. The speed to launch matters more than the marginal CVR gain here.

### 7.2 Tracking events

| Event | Trigger | Notes |

| PageView | Page load | Standard |
| Lead | Priority list form submit | **Primary optimisation event** |

**Critical:** Momence is a third party domain. Confirm whether a Meta pixel can be installed inside the Momence booking flow. If not, Purchase cannot be attributed and the campaign must optimise on Lead only, with membership sales reconciled manually.

## 8. Post-Conversion Flow

### 8.1 Thank you page

Do not send to a plain confirmation. The thank you page does three jobs:

1. **Confirm.** "You're on the list."
2. **Qualify.** Three optional questions, one screen: How often do you currently exercise? Have you tried reformer Pilates before? What time of day suits you best? These segment the nurture flow.
3. **Upsell.** "Still thinking about the Founding Membership? [X] of 10 remain." Link to Momence.

Fire the Lead event on page load here, not on button click.

### 8.2 Nurture sequence (outline)

| Timing | Message | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate | Welcome, what to expect, founding offer reminder with live count | Confirm and re-pitch |
| Day 1 | The studio and the method. Class types explained. | Educate, reduce intimidation |
| Day 3 | The maths. £10 a class vs everything else in Oxted. | Value case |
| Day 5 | Scarcity update. "[X] left." | Urgency |
| Day 8 | Social proof. Banstead member stories. | Trust |
| Day 12 | Fallback offer. Opening week early access or First Time Pass. | Convert the non-buyers |
| Ongoing | Opening date, timetable drop, booking window opens | Convert at launch |

SMS should run in parallel for the two highest urgency touches (scarcity update, booking window opens), given mobile numbers are being captured.

## 9. Risks and Assumptions

### Assumptions (flagged, need confirming)

- **A1.** The 200 credits are valid for 12 months from purchase date. If they run from the opening date instead, this must be stated clearly on the page, and it materially improves the offer.
- **A2.** Oxted will offer Barre and Yoga alongside Reformer and Mat. Taken from the current Oxted page copy.
- **A3.** Checkout remains on Momence with no payment plan option.
- **A4.** Founding credits are usable at Oxted only, not Banstead.
- **A5.** No Oxted-specific photography exists yet, so Banstead imagery will be used.

### Risks

- **Revenue ceiling.** 10 units at £2,000 caps direct return at £20,000. If media spend is material, judge the campaign on lead volume and lead quality, not on offer ROAS.
- **Sell-out dead zone.** If all 10 sell in week one and the page is not switched, the remaining spend drives traffic to an offer that no longer exists. Build the sold out state before launch, not after.
- **Price shock on cold traffic.** £2,000 upfront from a cold Meta audience is a very high bar. Expect the overwhelming majority of conversions on the priority list, and set the client's expectations accordingly before launch.
- **Buyer regret.** 200 classes in 12 months requires roughly four sessions a week, every week. Overselling this risks refund requests and negative word of mouth in a small town. Be honest in the FAQ.
- **Competitive response.** Very Studios and Studio Co are already trading in Oxted. Expect counter-offers. Speed to market on the founding offer matters.
- **Meta ad policy.** Fitness and wellness creative is subject to restrictions on personal attributes. No before and after, no body focused claims, no implied assumptions about the viewer's body. Brief creative accordingly.
- **Attribution gap.** If a pixel cannot be placed inside Momence, membership sales will not be attributable. Agree the manual reconciliation process with the client up front.
