# Landing Page Brief: Studio Glide Pilates, Oxted Priority List

**Client:** Studio Glide Pilates
**Project:** Oxted priority list capture page (no offer)
**Prepared by:** Vendo Digital
**Date:** 30 July 2026

> Companion to the Oxted Founding Membership brief. This page deliberately makes no mention of the £2,000 Founding Membership. Research, brand context and competitive analysis are covered in that document and are not repeated in full here.

---

## 1. Objective

**Single goal:** Capture local email addresses and mobile numbers onto the Oxted priority list.

There is no secondary CTA. No pricing. No offer. Nothing on this page competes with the form.

**Primary KPI:** Cost per lead
**Secondary KPI:** Lead quality, measured by postcode proximity and by lead to member conversion at opening

---

## 2. Why This Page Exists Alongside the Offer Page

The two pages do different jobs and should not be merged.

| | Founding Membership page | Priority list page (this one) |
|---|---|---|
| Traffic | Retargeting, warm, high intent | Broad cold prospecting |
| Ask | £2,000 upfront | An email address |
| Expected CPL | High | Low |
| Shelf life | Dead once 10 sell | Runs until opening and beyond |
| Risk | Price anchors people out | Weaker hook, needs a reason to sign up |

**Recommended split:** Run this page as the broad prospecting workhorse. Run the Founding Membership page to retargeting audiences and to lookalikes built off high value Banstead customers. The list this page builds becomes the retargeting pool for the offer page.

This page also becomes the fallback destination the moment all 10 memberships sell, with no rebuild required.

---

## 3. The Core Problem to Solve

Without an offer, "join our list" is a weak ask. People do not hand over a mobile number for a newsletter.

The page has to make joining the list feel like getting something, without naming a price or a discount.

### Recommended incentive stack (no offer disclosed)

**Default, recommended:**
1. **Early access booking.** Priority list members get the opening week timetable and booking link 72 hours before it goes public. Costs nothing. High perceived value in a market where boutique classes sell out.
2. **Opening offers first.** Frame as "anything we do for launch, the list hears about it first." True, specific enough to be credible, vague enough to avoid naming the Founding Membership.
3. **Opening intel.** Date, address, timetable, instructor line-up, delivered as it's confirmed.

**Optional test if CPL runs high:**
4. **Free taster class at opening**, list members only. Real cost per lead but a materially stronger hook. Worth testing as a split against the default once baseline CPL is established.

**Assumption A1:** The client can commit to a genuine 72 hour early access window before public booking opens. If not, remove point 1 and the page loses its strongest non-price hook. Confirm before build.

## 5. Design Requirements

- Match existing brand. Minimalist, generous white space, muted neutral palette, high quality photography.
- Mobile first. Assume 80%+ mobile traffic from Meta.
- Sticky CTA bar on scroll, anchoring to the form.
- No pricing anywhere on the page, including footer links. **Suppress the site's main navigation.** If this is built on Squarespace, the standard header links to /pricing, which undoes the entire premise of the page.
- Logo only in the header, no nav links. Minimal footer: contact email, privacy policy, terms.
- Target under 2.5s LCP on 4G.

**Hosting:** Same decision as the offer page. Squarespace on studioglidepilates.com/oxted-list is the fastest route and inherits the existing GTM container (GTM-TMPMWCSX). Requires nav suppression as noted above.

**Events:**

| Event | Trigger | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PageView | Page load | Standard |
| Lead | Thank you page load | **Primary optimisation event** |

## 7. Thank You Page

Three jobs, in this order.

1. **Confirm.** "You're on the list. Watch your inbox."
2. **Qualify.** Three optional questions on one screen: How often do you currently exercise? Have you tried reformer Pilates before? What time of day suits you best? These drive segmentation.
3. **Amplify.** Follow us on Instagram, plus a share prompt. No offer, no pricing, no Momence link.

Fire the Lead event here.

## 8. Nurture Sequence (outline)

Slower and softer than the offer page flow. These people have shown interest, not intent.

| Timing | Message | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate | Welcome. What to expect and when. | Confirm, set expectations |
| Day 2 | The studio and the method. Class types explained, intimidation removed. | Educate |
| Day 5 | Meet the team. Instructor introductions. | Build familiarity |
| Day 9 | Banstead member stories. | Social proof |
| Day 14 | Behind the scenes. Fit-out progress at Oxted. | Maintain attention |
| On confirmation | Opening date announced. | The moment that matters |
| Opening minus 7 days | Early access booking window opens. | Convert |
| Opening week | Timetable live, book now. | Convert |

**Segmentation trigger:** anyone who engages heavily (opens most emails, clicks through repeatedly) should be flagged as high intent and moved into the Founding Membership retargeting audience while stock remains.

SMS runs in parallel for two touches only: opening date announced, and early access booking opens.

## 9. Risks and Assumptions

**Assumptions**
- **A1.** A genuine 72 hour early access window can be honoured. Without it, remove that claim.
- **A2.** Oxted opening date is not yet publicly confirmed, so the page uses soft date language.
- **A3.** No Oxted photography exists, so Banstead imagery will be used and labelled as such.
- **A4.** This page will run alongside, not instead of, the Founding Membership page.

**Risks**
- **Weak hook without a price.** Expect a lower conversion rate than an offer page, offset by a far lower cost per click and a far broader addressable audience. Judge on CPL, not CVR.
- **Promise gap.** The page promises early access and opening offers. If neither materialises at opening, the list will disengage and the launch conversion collapses. This is a commitment from the client, not a copy device.
- **Nav leakage.** On Squarespace the default header will expose /pricing and the /oxted offer page. Must be suppressed or the page's premise fails.
- **Long lead time.** If opening is more than eight weeks away, expect list decay. The nurture sequence above is designed to counter this but the client must supply content, particularly fit-out progress.
- **Meta ad policy.** No before and after, no body focused claims, no implied assumptions about the viewer's body.
