Never done Pilates? That's where most people start. Maybe you've put it off because you're "not flexible enough" or think you've left it too late. GY3 · Low impact still hard work
Headline
Easy to follow. Felt tomorrow
Primary text
Low impact doesn't mean easy. If running hurts and the gym bores you, it's tempting to settle for a walk and call it exercise. Nothing wrong with a walk. But you want to feel like you've done something. Reformer Pilates uses springs and slow, controlled movement. No jumping, no pounding, and still a proper full-body workout. As one of our Banstead members put it: "easy to follow, but worked in all the right places and left me aching the next day!" First Time Pass: up to 6 classes for £60, used within 2 weeks. Reformer, Mat and Barre, 50 minutes, 6 to 8 people. New clients only. Tap Sign Up and book your first class on the Oxted timetable. Gentle on the joints. Not on the excuses.
