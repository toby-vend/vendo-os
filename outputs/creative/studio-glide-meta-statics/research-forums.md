# Studio Glide — forum quote bank (Meta research)

Collected 23 Oct 2026. Every quote below is verbatim, copied from the page text fetched from the URL shown, with original spelling and typos kept. `[…]` marks where I cut words out of a longer post. Mumsnet links go to the thread (page 1) where the post appears. Posters' usernames are left out.

Tags: **accommodation** (what they changed to cope) · **failed solution** · **nearly didn't** · **word** (their own words for the problem) · **moment** (trigger) · **after** (outcome in their words)

Source note: Reddit blocks both the browser and command-line fetches (it returns "Blocked"), so there are no Reddit quotes. Mumsnet worked and makes up most of the bank. One UK running blog (The Runner Beans) covers the ex-London persona. I left out posts that make medical or cure claims (sciatica, prolapse, arthritis and so on).
