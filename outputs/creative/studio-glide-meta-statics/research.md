## Client context (source: Studio Glide x Vendo catch-up call, 21 Sep 2026, Gemini notes in Drive)

- Oxted opened 21 August, late summer. Higher local competition and lower fill than Banstead.
- The studio is downstairs with no high-street visibility, so walk-ins are low. Ads have to do the finding.
- Oxted paid social leads cost about double Banstead's.
- Search split agreed at 70% Barre and Mat, 30% Reformer.
