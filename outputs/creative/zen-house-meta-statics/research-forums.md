# Zen House Dental: forum voice-of-customer research

Collected 9 October 2026. Every quote below is copied word for word from the live page, with the poster's original spelling, typos and emoji left in. `[…]` marks where I cut part of a longer post. The usernames are public Mumsnet handles.

**Sources:** all usable quotes came from Mumsnet Talk. I also tried the following sources, with these results:
- Reddit: blocked for both curl and WebFetch, so I skipped it.
- The Student Room: search only turned up chemistry threads, nothing relevant.
- MoneySavingExpert: only cost threads, with no first-person emotional content.
- DuckDuckGo: served a captcha.

**Caveat for Josh:** Mumsnet posters are mostly women, and many are older than 32. None of the bonding quotes clearly comes from a man aged 22 to 32. One poster (Arlanymor) played rugby but doesn't give a gender. Treat the Josh quotes as stand-ins for the right feelings, not an exact demographic match.
