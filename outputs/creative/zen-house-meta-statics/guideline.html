Our story

Zen House Dental was founded in 2021 by brother and sister Dr Shaimil Patel and Mrs Priya Shah, in the Banstead building that was once their parents' photo framing and printing studio.

In 2026 the brand opened a second practice on Northcote Road, Battersea. Both share one idea: replace dental anxiety with calm, through soft tones, luxury spaces and a personal touch.

02
Logo
the wordmark

Zen House Dental is a typographic wordmark: ZEN HOUSE in wide capitals over a tracked DENTAL. Always use the master artwork. Never retype it.

Clear space on every side equals the height of the Z. Minimum width is 120px on screen, 30mm in print.

06
Photography
real people, real rooms

Use the practice's own photography. Warm, bright and natural, with soft daylight and neutral rooms.

Three families of shot: team portraits on white in black scrubs; candid treatment moments (the mirror reveal, the scan, the aligner); and the two practices.

Smile close-ups always carry the ZEN HOUSE watermark and are used only with patient consent.

Avoid
Cold blue clinical stock, masked close-ups of instruments, before-and-afters without consent, and stock that shows another practice's rooms.

Tone of voice
how we sound
Calm, warm and quietly confident. Short sentences. UK English. No em dashes, no hype, no fear. Headlines lowercase and soft; the facts (prices, awards, reviews) do the persuading.
