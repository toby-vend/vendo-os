**Primary text:**
"Composite only lasts a couple of years. It chips easily."

You've probably read that. It's half true, so here's the whole answer.

With proper care, composite bonding typically lasts five to seven years before it needs a refresh, and individual teeth can last much longer. Biting hard things directly can chip it. Coffee and red wine can stain it over time.

What we do about it: unlike porcelain, bonding can be repaired if a chip happens, rather than replaced. We check it at your annual review. Every package comes with a FREE custom retainer to protect it and a FREE home whitening kit to keep the colour even.
