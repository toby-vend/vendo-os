## 2. Offer facts

### Invisalign (https://zenhousedental.co.uk/invisalign/)
- Free consultation, about 30 minutes, with an iTero 3D scan and a simulation of the expected result "before a single aligner is made".
- Every package includes home whitening, tooth contouring, 3 sets of Vivera retainers, a full dental assessment and OPG, and follow-ups at 3 and 6 months post-treatment.
- The bundle Toby confirmed live at both sites (9 Oct 2026, from Shaimil's 5 Sept "Imminent Offer" doc): free boutique whitening, free Vivera retainers, free check-up, free tooth sculpting, free iTero scan.
- Treatment time: "typically 6–18 months" in one place on the page and "12–18 months" in the FAQ. Conflict, so no duration claim on the ads.
- **Price conflict:** packages show two prices each (Lite £3,500/£3,000, Moderate £4,000/£3,500, Comprehensive £4,500/£4,000). The fees page says £1,900–£4,500. No Invisalign prices on the ads.
- 0% finance over 12 months, or Pay As You Go. No monthly figures on the ads (FCA).
- Aligners come out to eat, drink and brush.

### Composite bonding (https://zenhousedental.co.uk/composite-bonding/)
- No enamel removal, no drilling, reversible. Most cases take one visit; full-smile cases take 1–2 hours.
- Lasts 5–7 years with care. Can be repaired or touched up rather than replaced.
- Packages: 6 teeth £2,100, 8 teeth £2,800, 10 teeth £3,500. Each includes a dental exam, professional whitening, digital smile design, a final polish and a custom retainer. The fees page says £350 per tooth, which matches £2,100 ÷ 6.
- **Consultation conflict:** "A £30 non-refundable consultation fee applies", but the same page also links a "free smile consultation". Use "Book a consultation today" (the signed-off CTA) and never say free for bonding.
- Digital smile design: "so you can see your results before treatment even starts".

### Smile makeover (fees page, cosmetic pages)
- Smile makeover consultation: £30.
- Components: whitening £400, composite bonding £350 per tooth, porcelain veneers £950 per tooth, Invisalign (as above).
- No dedicated smile makeover price on the site.
